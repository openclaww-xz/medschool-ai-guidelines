# Amsterdam UMC — Artificial Intelligence (topic overview / news feed)

## (a) Authority and authorship
- Publisher: Amsterdam UMC (corporate communications), research-news topic page. Not a governing authority — this is an institution's public AI portfolio narrative.
- Coalition framing: "Amsterdam UMC is part of the coalition **AI Technology for People**" — the Amsterdam-region AI coalition (universities, hospitals, municipality) is the named institutional vehicle.

## (b) Policy philosophy
- Posture: **research-showcase, adoption-optimist**. Framing verbatim: "Computers can 'learn' from vast amounts of available data. In medicine, artificial intelligence (AI) is widely applicable: for example, **abnormalities can be found on scans that cannot be seen with the naked eye**. Amsterdam UMC is part of the coalition AI Technology for People to **make full use of the possibilities of AI**."
- Zero risk language, zero governance language on this page — pure opportunity narrative.

No normative clauses; the page is a dated news feed. Full inventory:

7. **July 22, 2024 — European study on OR staff burnout**: ~60% of perioperative caregivers showing burnout symptoms; "up to half of nurses considering leaving"; Horizon grant of almost €6.5M; Amsterdam UMC leads consortium.

9. **February 7, 2024 — Predicting antidepressant response**: major depression; "predict within a week whether an antidepressant will work" via brain scan + clinical information into an AI algorithm (Amsterdam UMC + Radboudumc), "up to 8 weeks faster"; published in American Journal of Psychiatry.
