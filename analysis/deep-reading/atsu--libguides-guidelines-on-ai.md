# ATSU — Guidelines on AI (LibGuide best-practices page)

## Authority / Authorship
- A.T. Still University library guide.

## Complete clause inventory
1. "Do not use generative AI applications to generate clinical documentation, such as medical records or letters."
2. "Avoid utilizing generative AI applications with any form of protected health information or other legally protected data."
3. **"Refrain from employing generative AI applications for clinical or human subjects research, even if the data has been anonymized."**
4. Keep confidential business information away.
5. Copyright resource stack (Blueprint for an AI Bill of Rights; Library Copyright Alliance principles — "training generative AI models on copyrighted works is fair use" (LCA position); AI-created-art moral rights).

## Classification
- **cautious-informed (library layer)** — the anonymized-clinical-research ban is stricter than most (rejects even de-identified research uses of public AI); lean four-clause core with a copyright-reading list.
