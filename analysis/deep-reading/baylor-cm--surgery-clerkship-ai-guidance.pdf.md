# Baylor CM — Surgery Core Clerkship AI Guidance (May 2025)

## Authority / Authorship
- BCM Surgery Core Clerkship Course Overview Document (v. 2025-05); clerkship-level, references college-level SOM policies on Blackboard.

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- "Microsoft Copilot is the BCM-recommended AI tool... when a BCM student/employee logs into Copilot with BCM username and password, entered data is not used to train the AI model... This is not true for other open platforms. Copilot is NOT HIPAA or FERPA compliant."
- Educational integration: "Generative AI may be incorporated as a supplementary learning tool to support existing curriculum."

## Complete clause inventory
1. Technical definition block (LLM/GPT; AI as "a suite of computational methods—e.g., machine learning (ML), natural language processing...").
2. Tool security facts: BCM Copilot = no training on inputs, no retention; still not HIPAA/FERPA compliant → "inputting identifiable patient information into any AI tool is NOT allowed."
3. Acceptable clerkship uses: "refining illness scripts during pre-rounding, broadening a differential diagnosis prior to presenting a patient, and stimulating discussion during didactics."
4. Disclosure norm: "Use of AI tools should be properly cited (e.g., if you used AI to help you create your differential, state that when you present to your attending/resident)."
5. "Submitting work contributed to by an AI tool for a course requirement or for a grade is prohibited unless specifically permitted in the Course Overview Document (COD)."
6. "Using AI for medical documentation in the EHR is strictly prohibited unless otherwise specified in the COD."
7. PHI ban with enumerated identifiers (name, DOB, pictures, MRN).
8. "Any prohibited use of AI is grounds for an Honor Council investigation."
9. Named prohibited examples: H&Ps in EHR, written Peds H&P, reflection assignment after failed SP case.
10. Professionalism consequences: UME grading committee, MSPE reporting, Ethicspoint.

## Sophistication markers
- Named secure tool with precise security properties (training, retention) AND its compliance limits stated honestly.
- Verbal-disclosure-at-presentation norm — oral citation culture, not just written.
- Worked prohibited examples tied to specific clerkship assessments.

## Distinctives
- Clerkship-granular policy (rare); the attending-presentation disclosure is a unique clause in the corpus.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured** — provisioned tool + scenario-level rules + Honor-Council enforcement; contained by COD-permission default.
