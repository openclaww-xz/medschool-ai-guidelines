# Brown — Announcing AI Resources & Course Policy Tools (Aug 18, 2026)

## Authority / Authorship
- Provost Francis J. Doyle III; GAITL Phase 2 Committee (co-chairs Michael Littman, associate provost for AI; Ravit Reichman); Sheridan Center execution.

## Clause inventory
1. Problem framing: "Our students are currently navigating a complex landscape where AI expectations vary significantly from one classroom to the next."
2. Mandate-by-nudge: "It is essential that every instructor at Brown sets a clear, explicit AI policy for their courses."
3. "I encourage you not only to state what is permitted, but also to discuss the pedagogical 'why' with your students."
4. Deliverables: Syllabus Policy Examples ("from a strict 'No AI' policy to assignment-specific integration") and **AI Policy Explorer** — "an interactive web tool... Based on your input, the tool will automatically generate a customizable syllabus statement."
5. Rooted in the campus GenAI in Teaching and Learning report (near-term recommendation #1).

## Sophistication markers
- A policy-statement *generator tool* — the guidance layer industrializing syllabus-policy production.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured (faculty-enablement layer)** — Brown governs by tooling and example rather than rule; consistent with its report-first culture.
