## Clause inventory

3. Acceptable Use of IT Policy.
4. EIT Accessibility Policy.
5. Security Review requirement for new/existing systems incl. AI.
6. Student Code of Conduct + **Export Control Regulations** ("The use of AI must comply with Export Control Regulations") — rare clause.
7. Employee conduct, COI ("AI may not be used in a manner that creates a conflict of interest"), harassment policy ("AI may not be used to engage in any form of harassment") — behavior-specific AI extensions rare in this layer.
