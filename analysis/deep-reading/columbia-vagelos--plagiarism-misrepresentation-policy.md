# columbia-vagelos — plagiarism-misrepresentation-policy

# Columbia VP&S — Plagiarism and Misrepresentation (student policies)

## Authority / Authorship
- VP&S Office of Medical Education; LCME 3.5 cited; cross-referenced to CUIMC Cares values, Honor Code, Standards of Discipline.

## Clause inventory
1. Patient-safety rationale: "Plagiarism and misrepresentation rightfully erode the trust of patients in our doctors."
2. General prohibitions (cheating, plagiarize, falsify, assist).
3. Medical-record accuracy duty: "Student work should be original and may not be copied and pasted without attribution. This applies to admission notes, progress notes, discharge summaries, written papers, scholarly work and personal statements for residency."
4. **AI clause**: "Absent a clear statement from a course instructor granting permission, the use of Generative AI tools to complete an assignment or exam is prohibited. The unauthorized use of AI shall be treated similarly to unauthorized assistance and/or plagiarism."
5. "If allowed, when using AI, students are encouraged to discuss expectations with instructors and ensure that any use of generative AI in their work is clearly cited."
6. "VPS students are expected to comply with the University Generative AI policy."
7. Enforcement: Honor Code, Dean's Discipline, Academic Infraction Policies.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — prohibition-default with clean university-policy piggyback; note the interesting inversion — the SOM layer is *stricter* than the permissive-encouraging university layer above it.
