# Columbia — University Generative AI Policy

## Authority / Authorship
- Office of the Provost; "AI Team" working group of faculty + senior administrators; explicitly "a 'work in progress' as the technology, the law and the Columbia community usage evolves."

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- "Columbia University is dedicated to advancing knowledge and learning, and embraces generative AI tools... We encourage you to explore and experiment with these tools."

## Complete clause inventory
1. Procure via CUIT/CUIMC-IT vendor management (incl. free tools).
2. "must not input any Confidential Information into Generative AI tools, except when permitted by validated contract language and security controls" (Confidential defined to include PHI).
3. Same for Personal Information (expansive definition).
4. IP/contract-term clause: users must respect IP in inputs and outputs; library-licensed content may forbid AI use (ai-inquiries@library.columbia.edu).
5. Accuracy-verification duty (hallucinations).
6. Bias-check duty: "must consider whether the data input into, and the output of, Generative AI tools produces decisions that may result in a disparate impact... Do not rely on any output that is indicative of a potential bias."
7. Disclosure duty: "must disclose that those materials and that work product is based on or derives from the use of Generative AI."
8. No passing off AI output as own; no plagiarizing via AI.
9. Malicious-content prohibition (malware etc.).
10. **Opt-out clause**: "Where that option is available, Columbia community members are expected to exercise it and opt out of training."
11. Approved-applications list + exceptions via AIpolicy@columbia.edu.
12. Students: ask instructors; if instructor prohibits, "unauthorized use of AI shall be treated similarly to unauthorized assistance and/or plagiarism."
13. Instructors: clear syllabus AI statements; sample statements; report suspected violations promptly; "a report from an AI detection tool... can not be used as the sole piece of proof in a Dean's Discipline case."
14. Research: transparency in methods/acknowledgments; fabricated-citation warning; no unpublished data into tools; journal/sponsor policy compliance.

## Sophistication markers
- Disparate-impact bias duty (civil-rights framing); opt-out-of-training expectation; anti-sole-proof detection rule; library-license AI carve-out.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured** — the most complete university-level policy in the guidance cohort; VP&S adds plagiarism-prohibition and deferral on top (separate note).
