# CU SOM — Artificial Intelligence Guidelines of the MD Program

## Authority / Authorship
- University of Colorado School of Medicine; excerpt from MD Program Curriculum Steering Committee, Trek Policies & Procedures AY 2026-27 p.17; contact Matt Zuckerman, MD.

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- "CUSOM supports the responsible, transparent, and ethical use of AI to enhance learning and patient care while preserving academic integrity, clinical reasoning, and human judgment."

## Complete clause inventory
1. "AI is assistive, not a replacement" — never replace "independent clinical reasoning, required skill development, or professional judgment."
2. "Human responsibility remains paramount."
3. Transparency: substantive use must be disclosed (tool + how); routine grammar/spelling exempt; worked disclosure example ("ChatGPT‑4o was used to generate an initial outline and first draft... All content was subsequently revised, verified, and finalized by the student.").
4. "Graduated use across training": "Early learners are expected to develop foundational skills independently before using AI for augmentation."
5. Faculty/preceptor authority to restrict/permit/structure.
6. Permitted uses: study aids; outlines/clarity with disclosure; comparing own documentation to AI examples; "Brainstorming differential diagnoses or care considerations after independent reasoning."
7. Prohibited: undisclosed AI submission; exams; "Entering patient, student, or confidential institutional information into unapproved AI tools"; "Using AI before meeting required competency milestones where independent performance is expected."
8. Data privacy: PHI/HIPAA, FERPA, institutional data; approved systems only.

## Sophistication markers
- Competency-milestone gating ("before meeting required competency milestones") — developmental-stage AI policy; disclosure exemplar inline.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured (near pioneer)** — a genuinely pedagogical guidance document: milestone logic, differential-after-independent-reasoning sequencing, model disclosure statement.
