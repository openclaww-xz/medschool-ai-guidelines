# The 'DeepSeek Effect' and the Adoption–Integration Gap: Chinese Critical Care Physicians (Frontiers in Medicine, 2026)

## Research question
Did the DeepSeek release (Jan 2025) change GAI adoption among Chinese academic critical care physicians, and does adoption translate into professional integration? Does structured training help?

## Methods
Two cross-sectional WeChat/conference convenience surveys: pre-DeepSeek Dec 2024 (n=456) vs post Dec 2025 (n=372), 31 provinces; 40-item instrument with psychometric validation (α=0.925–0.959, CFA fit OK, S-CVI 0.986); χ²/Mann-Whitney; weighted-ranking RII for drivers; multivariable logistic regression; 3-dimension definition of "structured training."

## Sample
828 academic critical care physicians (tertiary hospitals; ~63% attending).

## Key findings (with counts)
- Adoption: 64.7% → 94.1% (p<0.001); use in medical education 33.1% → 52.7%; daily use 21.4% → 34.0%. Model shift: DeepSeek-family 34.3% primary; US models 10.5% → 2.2%.
- Drivers (RII): domestic model capability 37.2%, accessibility/usability 31.6% (together 68.8%); institutional policy guidance ranked LAST at 2.2%.
- Adoption–integration gap: perceived professional competence unchanged (61.0% vs 56.2% reporting increase, p=0.16) despite near-universal adoption.
- Training: only 13.7% had any GAI training; of those, just 15/51 met "structured" criteria (3 dimensions). Any training: AOR 1.98 for perceived competence gain; structured training AOR 22.2 (95% CI 1.6–305.6 — hypothesis-generating only, n=15).
- Structured-training physicians prioritized critical appraisal over tool operation (86.7% vs 28.6%, OR 16.3, p<0.001).
- Trust scenario-dependent: falls when GAI contradicts clinical judgment (50.4%→42.2%), rises when no prior judgment exists (56.8%→65.3%). Ethical concerns (over-reliance 86%, privacy ~80%) unchanged across waves.

## What it says about WHO leads policy
Stark finding: institutional policy guidance was the WEAKEST adoption driver (2.2%) — adoption is technology-led, not policy-led. Recommendations assign institutions governance protocols, AI-assisted vs doctor-led role definition, and structured training; parallels INSIGHT-AI France (63% adoption, 7.8% trained).

## Limitations
Convenience sample of digitally engaged academics; n=15 structured-training subgroup; self-reported competence only; cross-sectional cohorts, not longitudinal individuals.

## Corroboration/contradiction with our corpus
Provides the quantitative mechanism for what our corpus shows structurally: policy follows adoption, not the reverse — matching the Clin Teach gap study's timeline (policies codifying already-established student use). The 94%-adopt/14%-trained combination is the strongest published expression of the implementation gap our corpus documents. Also a sovereignty datapoint: domestic-model substitution occurred within a year where US models were inaccessible — an adoption-driver absent from US-centric policy.
