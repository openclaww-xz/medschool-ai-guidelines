# Drexel IT — GenAI Guidance

## Authority / Authorship
- Drexel University Information Technology (security policies section).

## Clause inventory
1. Definitions (ChatGPT/Gemini as exemplars).
2. "any university sensitive data must not be shared with any of the consumer versions of these GenAI services that have not been reviewed and approved for use."
3. Cross-border processing risk: "it’s possible it might allow prompts and attached files to be processed in other countries, where compliance with U.S. law covering regulated data (e.g., academic records covered by FERPA or health records covered by HIPAA) may not be enforced."
4. Approved services governed by agreements protecting IP/privacy.
5. New-service requests: risk assessment via Information Security; multi-office consultation (Provost, Compliance/Policy/Privacy, OGC, Research & Innovation).
6. External reference list (NIST, Public Voice universal guidelines, 2023 WH EO, NSA guidance, IAPP).

## Sophistication markers
- Foreign-processing/jurisdictional risk clause — rare; concrete multi-office procurement path.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — compact IT guidance with one genuinely unusual clause (extraterritorial data processing).
