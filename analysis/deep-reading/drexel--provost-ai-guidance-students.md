# Drexel Provost — AI Guidance for Students

## Authority / Authorship
- Drexel Office of the Provost (Pedagogy & Guidance series); operationalizes PO 103 §IV-C for students; companion Dragons' Guide from the Teaching & Learning Center.

## Clause inventory
1. Know the rules: "Every course may have different rules about AI use... If you're unsure, ask before using AI tools."
2. "Cite Your AI Use... If no citation style is given, use the one common in your field. See Appendix B (Section VI-B of PO 103)."
3. "Own Your Work... you are accountable for what you submit"; violations to Student Conduct & Care; "misuse of AI tools may result in loss of access to AI services."
4. Protect info: never enter student ID, grades, education records, health info, contacts, confidential third-party info; "If you wouldn't post it publicly... don't put it into an AI tool."
5. Do-list: grammar/polish, brainstorm/outline, planning & executive-function support (accessibility angle), verify outputs, follow course rules.
6. Don't-list: replace critical thinking; submit AI work as own; "In most cases, AI should not be used to produce final versions of assignments beyond proofreading"; trust AI citations; skip required disclosure.

## Sophistication markers
- Learning-support framing including ADHD/executive-function uses — accessibility-positive stance rare in this layer.
- Explicit pointer into numbered policy sections (PO 103 §IV-C, §VI-B) — guidance welded to enacted policy.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured** — student-facing layer of a real policy (PO 103); practical, warm, precise.
