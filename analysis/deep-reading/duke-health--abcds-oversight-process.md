# Duke Health — ABCDS Oversight Process

## Authority / authorship
- **Algorithm-Based Clinical Decision Support (ABCDS) Oversight** — a dedicated, named Duke Health program with its own domain (healthaigovernance.duke.edu), an AI Oversight Policy, a Standard Operating Procedure, an Implementation Subcommittee, independent Review Committees, office hours, and a registry. Authorship is institutional (the program), clinically anchored (Duke's documented work on algorithmic clinical decision support safety).

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- Registry-first: "ABCDS Oversight manages a registry of all algorithmic solutions under consideration for use in patient care at Duke Health."
- Lifecycle checkpoints: "an independent Review Committee is assigned to perform evaluations at checkpoints throughout the lifecycle — from procurement or development to general deployment, monitoring, and maintenance."

## Sophistication markers
- **Clinical deployment governance (approval pipeline): the most developed in this run** — mandatory registry of ALL patient-care algorithms; tri-partite risk stratification (full/fast-track/registration-only); independent review committees (independence from the project team is explicit); stage-gated lifecycle checkpoints "from procurement or development to general deployment, monitoring, and maintenance"; four-way decision outcomes incl. conditional approval and decline; published templates/checklists.
- **Monitoring:** checkpoints explicitly extend through "monitoring, and maintenance" — post-deployment surveillance is inside the review mandate, not an afterthought. (Operational details live in the SOP, not captured here.)
- **Model-failure handling:** "Approval with contingencies" and "Re-Review" are conditional-continuation mechanisms; gap-resolution timelines required on non-approval. No incident-reporting text on this page.
- **Ambient-scribe consent:** not addressed (algorithm oversight, not documentation tools).
- **Clinician liability:** low-risk tools delegate validation to named "Clinical and Model Owners" — accountability is named-role-based.
- Bias: dedicated Bias Management Framework; regulatory: FDA SaMD/Digital Health Navigator integration.

## Distinctives
- Registry + risk-tiered pathways + independent committees + stage gates — the closest thing to a regulator-style pipeline in the corpus.
- Public office hours and email intake — operationalized accessibility for proposers.
- Named instrument set (Oversight Policy, SOP, templates, checklists, Bias Management Framework) all published on a dedicated governance site.

## Provisional classification
**Pioneer (evaluation-and-monitoring tier).** Duke is the corpus's clearest case of governance built specifically for clinical algorithm deployment with lifecycle monitoring in scope. The page is a process description rather than a policy, but the machinery (registry, tiers, independent review, stage gates) is concrete and referenced-in-practice.
