# Emory — AI Content Generation & Proofing Guidelines

## Authority / Authorship
- Emory responsibleai.emory.edu task-specific guidance series.

## Clause inventory
1. Context verification first: coursework (syllabi/handbooks), research (journal AI policies — Nature/JAMA/NEJM named), admin (leadership/legal/OIT consult), clinical ("must comply with patient privacy laws (e.g., HIPAA) and Emory Healthcare guidelines"; OGC for PHI questions).
2. "Use only Emory-Approved Secure AI Tools" (EASAT registry).
3. Review practices: cross-check edits; retain originals to compare/reverse.
4. "Disclose AI involvement where relevant or required."
5. Embedded-AI discernment: "If you are uncertain, treat any tool that suggests or rewrites content as potentially AI-enhanced."
6. Disclosure thresholds: required in specific scenarios (journal articles, legal mandates) but "generally not necessary to disclose AI use for internal drafting, brainstorming, or minor content refinement"; unit-level thresholds encouraged.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured** — a tiered disclosure logic (context-dependent, unit-calibrated) more nuanced than most formal policies.
