# Emory — Responsible AI Guidelines and Principles

## Authority / Authorship
- Emory University (responsibleai.emory.edu); "developed through multiple rounds of input from across the Emory community."

## Clause inventory
1. Mission alignment ("create, preserve, teach, and apply knowledge in the service of humanity").
2. Legal adherence: "Prevent and detect potential academic, research, funding, or medical misconduct involving AI"; update policies; respect third-party AI policies.
3. Transparency: "Inform users when interacting with AI and enable consent where possible"; "Label AI-generated content"; "Provide clear rationales for AI-guided decisions."
4. Fairness: accessibility for all users; "Identify and eliminate inherent and explicit bias in AI-supported decisions, especially in critical areas like academics, healthcare, and research."
5. Safety/Security/Privacy.
6. Universal accountability clause.
7. **Self-disclosure**: "In the spirit of disclosure, we share that AI assisted with editing and proofreading this message, but the ideas, consensus process, final wording, and accountability all come from the team."
8. Ecosystem: AI.Humanity initiative, CAIL (Center for AI Learning), AI.Health institute.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured** — principle layer with consent + labeling duties and a practice-what-you-preach disclosure; the three named institutes show research-infrastructure depth.
