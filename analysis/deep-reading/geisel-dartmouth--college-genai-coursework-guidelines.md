# Dartmouth College — Guidelines on using GenAI for Coursework (038-0018)

## Authority / Authorship
- Dartmouth Office of the Provost; Policy ID 038-0018; Effective July 1, 2024; Last Revised Sept 2, 2025; Next Review 2027; secondary responsibility: Office of Community Standards & Accountability. Scope: "All AB undergraduates and special non-degree seeking students" (Geisel MD students covered separately by UME-CNTRL-0019, run 27).

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- "Dartmouth recognizes emergent Generative Artificial Intelligence technologies as powerful tools for learning."
- Default rule: "Dartmouth assumes the general rule that GenAI tools are disallowed by default in courses to permit instructors full autonomy."

## Complete clause inventory
1. "Students may not use GenAI tools for submitted coursework unless expressly permitted."
2. Instructors are the experts; may allow or disallow per course/assignment.
3. Faculty "strongly encouraged" to define GenAI policies in syllabus; instructor policy governs under Academic Honor Policy.
4. Acknowledgment required whenever permitted; "As GenAI applications are not stable, reproducible sources, in most cases use of these tools will not be referenced or cited in the same way as conventional scholarly sources."
5. Default citation fallback: discipline-appropriate method if instructor gives none; "Failure to acknowledge or cite GenAI use may result in disciplinary action."
6. Student duty to clarify with instructor.
7. Three verbatim sample acknowledgment statements (Claude via DartmouthChat with prompt text; ChatGPT 5 with public link; GPT-5 outline with version/date).

## Sophistication markers
- The "not stable, reproducible sources" rationale for a distinct acknowledgment (vs citation) regime — epistemically precise.
- Sample statements include model, version, date, prompt, and public chat URL.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed (near progressive)** — default-deny for undergraduates, but with the most careful disclosure-craft in the cohort; Geisel's MD-level policy (run 27) is the stricter sibling.
