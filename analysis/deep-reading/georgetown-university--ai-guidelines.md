# Georgetown — AI Guidelines (University Information Services)

## Authority / Authorship
- Developed by University Information Services "in consultation with the Office of General Counsel and with many members of the university community beginning in January 2024." Explicitly: "These guidelines are not intended as legal advice or an exhaustive set of best practices."

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- "The university recognizes there is transformative potential of AI but also recognizes that as we strive to leverage its capabilities, we must adhere to Georgetown’s core principles, institutional values, and existing policies."

## Clause inventory
1. Bot rule: "Do not allow unfamiliar bots to join your meetings."
2. "Do not upload, input, or analyze university data in an AI tool that is not approved by UIS and under an enterprise-wide contract."
3. Six principles, each with expand-links: Accountability ("accountable for the work product or decisions resulting from their use of AI"); Transparency ("disclosed if prescribed by law, university policy, or publisher requirements"); Fairness ("outputs may sometimes contain bias"); Privacy; Security; IP & Confidentiality.
4. Explainer layer: What is AI/GenAI/LLM/use-cases.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — IT+OGC consensus guidelines; principle scaffold with enterprise-contract gate; no academic or clinical substance (deferred to schools).
