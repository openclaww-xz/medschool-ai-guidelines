# Harvard HUIT — Generative AI Guidelines

## Authority / Authorship
- Harvard University Information Technology; the maintained (periodically updated) version of the provost initial guidelines.

## Clause inventory (deltas & emphases vs 2023 provost letter)
1. Confidential-data prohibition restated with expanded enumeration: "finance, HR, student records, medical information, etc."
2. "Level 2 and above confidential data must only be entered into generative AI tools that have been assessed and approved for such use."
3. Review-before-publishing responsibility.
4. "Adhere to local academic and administrative policies" — School-level policy links.
5. Phishing/deepfake alert ("video or audio intended to convincingly mimic a person's voice or physical appearance without their consent").
6. Approved-tools regime: contractual protections, "assurances that the data entered will not be used to train vendor models."
7. AI meeting assistants banned in Harvard meetings except approved tools; AI Assistant Guidelines linked.
8. EU AI Act prohibited-use-cases awareness link.

## Sophistication markers
- Deepfake social-engineering clause; EU AI Act awareness; meeting-assistant governance; vendor no-training contractual assurance as standard.
