# HMS Academic & Research Integrity — AI in Research

## Authority / Authorship
- HMS Academic and Research Integrity office; a link-hub with embedded best-practice clauses.

## Clause inventory
1. Do "not enter data classified as confidential (Level 2 and above), including non-public research data, into publicly-available generative AI tools."
2. "AI Tools cannot be listed as an author on a paper."
3. NIH: peer reviewers prohibited from LLMs for critiques; NSF: no uploading proposal content to non-approved tools (both linked).
4. COPE/WAME authorship links.
5. ORI guidance on AI in research-misconduct proceedings (2026) linked.
6. NIH genomic-data AI notice (NOT-OD-25-081).
7. Tools table: "Level 3 data and below" for AI Sandbox, ChatGPT Edu, AI Meeting Assistants, Firefly, Gemini, Copilot, Claude.
