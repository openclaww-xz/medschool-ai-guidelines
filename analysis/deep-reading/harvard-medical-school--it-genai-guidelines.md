# HMS IT — Generative AI Guidelines

## Authority / Authorship
- Harvard Medical School IT; "builds on Initial guidelines for the use of Generative AI tools at Harvard."

## Clause inventory
1. Verify outputs ("may be incorrect or fabricated. Always fact-check before using or publishing").
2. Uphold academic integrity per handbooks; "Be transparent with students and colleagues about acceptable use."
3. "Cite AI contributions appropriately in research and academic work. Refer to guidance from COPE and WAME."
4. "Do not enter confidential information (Level 2 and above) into public-facing AI platforms."
5. Data-level by tool: Level 1 public tools; Level 2 meeting assistants (Zoom Companion, Copilot Premium in meetings); Level 3 Harvard AI Sandbox + Claude/ChatGPT Edu/Copilot; Level 4 special-request (Longwood Cluster, HMS Azure AI); "Level 5 — No AI tool is approved."
6. DUA clause: "Even if an AI tool is approved for your data's security level, a Data Use Agreement (DUA) may separately restrict a dataset's use" — consult ORA Research Data Compliance.
7. Compliance: HIPAA, FERPA, sponsor-specific; NIH/COPE alignment.
8. Tool table (8 tools × use-cases × data level × eligibility × training-required × cost); "one AI tool — Claude, ChatGPT Edu, or Microsoft Copilot Premium — per eligible person"; Quad-based eligibility (MD/DMD students included).
9. Procurement support; phishing reporting.

## Sophistication markers
- Five-level data classification mapped to a named tool inventory — the most granular security-to-tool mapping in the guidance corpus.
- DUA-overrides-tool-approval clause: data governance literacy beyond most policies.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured** — exemplary medical-school IT guidance layer; pedagogy deferred to handbooks.
