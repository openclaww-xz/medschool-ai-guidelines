# Harvard Provost — Initial Guidelines for ChatGPT & GenAI Tools

## Authority / Authorship
- Signed Alan M. Garber (Provost), Meredith Weenick (EVP), Klara Jelinkova (VP/CIO). The canonical 2023 university-wide initial guidelines.

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- "The University supports responsible experimentation with generative AI tools."
- "It is important to note that these guidelines are not new University policy; rather, they leverage existing University policies."

## Clause inventory
1. "You should not enter data classified as confidential (Level 2 and above), including non-public research data, into publicly-available generative AI tools" (Information Security Policy).
2. "You are responsible for any content that you produce or publish that includes AI-generated material" — hallucination + copyright warning; review before publication.
3. Adhere to School academic-integrity policies; "faculty should be clear with students... about their policies on permitted uses, if any"; students encouraged to ask.
4. AI-enabled phishing alert; report to phishing@harvard.
5. "Connect with HUIT before procuring generative AI tools"; vendor tools "must be assessed for risk by Harvard's Information Security and Data Privacy office prior to use."

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — the archetypal university scaffold: data-levels + procurement + integrity-deferral to Schools. No medical-school-specific content; HMS layer (separate captures) sits on top.
