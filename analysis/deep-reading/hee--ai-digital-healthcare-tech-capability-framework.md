# HEE — AI and Digital Healthcare Technologies Capability Framework (landing page)

## (a) Authority and authorship
- Publisher: NHS Health Education England (now NHS England Workforce, Planning and Education Functions after HEE merger). Not a rule-maker: HEE is the workforce-training arm of the NHS; this is a capability framework, not binding regulation.
- Authorship is outsourced and disclosed: "Health Education England (HEE) **commissioned the University of Manchester to perform a learning needs analysis and develop a framework** outlining the skills and capabilities to ensure our health and care professionals can work in a digitally enhanced environment."
- Audience: the entire English health and care workforce ("our healthcare workforce"), not just medicine or undergraduates.

## (b) Policy philosophy
- Posture: **capability-building, not restriction**. The single framing sentence is adaptationist: "A clear need for our healthcare workforce is to **continually adapt to meet the needs of the society it serves**."
- The page itself is a navigation shell (Foreword / Introduction / Methodology and framework theory / Framework / Applying the framework and next steps); all substantive content lives in sibling pages, two of which are captured separately in this corpus (structure page and the long "news" page).

## (c) Complete clause inventory
The landing page contains no normative clauses. Its complete content inventory:
1. Title: "Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Digital Healthcare Technologies Capability framework".
2. Needs statement: workforce "continually adapt" clause quoted above.
3. Commissioning clause: HEE commissioned University of Manchester for learning-needs analysis + framework development (verbatim in (a)).
4. Navigation links: "Read about the authors and the foreword"; "An introduction to Artificial Intelligence (AI) and the capability framework"; "Read about the methodology and framework"; "Find out more information about the framework structure"; "How to apply the framework structure and the next steps".
- No permissions, prohibitions, disclosure rules, or sanctions appear on this page.

## (d) Sophistication markers
- Structural marker: a national workforce body commissioning an external university learning-needs analysis as the evidence base for a capability framework — governance-by-competency rather than governance-by-rule.
- Technically generic on this page (by design; detail lives in the framework-structure and news pages).

## (e) Distinctives vs sibling frameworks
- This is the only document family in the international corpus that governs by **capability statements** (195 of them, per the structure page) rather than by policy clauses — a curriculum instrument, not a conduct instrument.
- Positions AI as one of six digital domains (implementation, direct care, ethics/law, human factors, data management, AI) — AI is embedded in a wider digital-literacy edifice rather than siloed.

## (f) Provisional classification
**progressive-structured** — national-scale, methodologically grounded (needs analysis → framework → application guidance), permissive-adaptationist in posture, with zero enforcement apparatus. It assumes AI adoption and works backward to workforce capability.
