# Hofstra — AI Policy for Faculty and Students (Provost)

## Authority / Authorship
- Hofstra Office of the Provost; anchored in Faculty Policy Series #11 and the Honor Code.

## Complete clause inventory
1. Value: "AI technologies... are integral to academic and professional disciplines"; "appropriate ethical use will change over time."
2. Faculty discretion: "instructors have broad discretion to determine the appropriate use of AI tools."
3. Syllabus duty: "The instructor should provide a clear statement about AI utilization in their course syllabus"; discuss at course start.
4. Students: read/abide by syllabus statements; "If the instructor does not provide guidance, students should cite content derived from AI tools using the style most closely resembling the course or department's general academic submission format."
5. "All final works are the sole responsibility of the student."
6. Library databases approved for coursework unless specified.
7. **AI-detection evidence rule**: "faculty should not rely on AI detection as the sole source of evidence in cases of suspected academic misconduct"; corroborating-evidence list (writing-style inconsistency, sudden improvement, off-class content, prompt mismatch, unusual vocabulary).

## Classification
- **progressive-structured** — compact, balanced, with a practical multi-evidence corroboration list for misconduct; ChatGPT Edu provisioned campus-wide (see overview capture).
