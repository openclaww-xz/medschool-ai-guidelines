# Howard — Initial Guidelines for Use of Generative AI Tools (Provost)

## Authority / Authorship
- Howard University Provost's Office; the pre-policy "initial guidelines" essay (predates/supplements Policy 100-022).

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- "Writing and voice production remain important modes of critical, artistic, and sense-based learning vital to understanding and cognitive development."
- Unique equity framing: "AI technologies echo dominant viewpoints of certain groups, spread misinformation... Artificial intelligence models or other content-generating tools fail to grasp the diverse nuances of human opinion, thought, language, etc…especially within the African Diaspora."
- "Howard University prepares students to be leaders, and our faculty engage and prepare students to be the architects and builders of new technologies, not solely the users."

## Clause inventory
1. AGI-vs-GenAI disambiguation (Terminator/Data framing).
2. Values grounding in Howard Core Values (African Diaspora impact).
3. Bias-mitigation duty: "all stakeholders should be involved in mitigating such bias as a major priority."
4. Privacy: "assume that information shared with generative AI tools is not private."
5. Embedded-AI awareness (M365, Google Docs).
6. AI-literacy demand "across colleges, disciplines, departments, and programs."
7. Decentralized policy-making: "Schools and colleges, as well as departments and faculty should develop their own policies... There will likely be no one size fits all approach" — but "there should be a clear and understood set of parameters... that clearly outline penalties."
8. Scraping/fabrication warning; citation-culture obligation.
9. Academic dishonesty: submitting "substantially created" AI work without permission is a concern; instructor may inquire into authorship; "selected students also have the liberty to defend themselves against such allegations. Fairness and transparency are key."
10. Phishing/cybersecurity caution.

## Sophistication markers
- The only guidance document in the corpus with an explicit demographic-equity epistemology (bias against African Diaspora discourse).
- Due-process symmetry (inquiry ↔ defense) stated at guidance level.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured** — values-rich, equity-centered, deliberately decentralized; later formalized in Policy 100-022.
