# JHU — Generative AI Guidelines (Teaching @ JHU, university teaching-policies)

## Authority / Authorship
- JHU university teaching-policies site (teaching.jhu.edu); FERPA/HIPAA/IP/ethics sections + example syllabi from named instructors across divisions (KSAS, SAIS, Peabody, School of Education); last updated 2/19/26.

## Clause inventory
1. **FERPA**: before submitting student work to AI tools, proper de-identification "requires removal of all personally identifiable information, as well as a reasonable determination... that a student's identity is not personally identifiable," incl. personal-experience content.
2. **HIPAA**: use with health information "only with JH-approved vendors where appropriate agreements are in place"; re-identification risk even after de-identification.
3. **IP**: U.S. Copyright Office human-authorship rule quoted; vendor ToS may claim output ownership; check tool policies.
4. **Ethics**: bias, transparency, consent, "social shame" of AI reliance (with mitigation strategies).
5. Student guidelines: Validation / Inclusiveness / Communication / Transparency.
6. Six verbatim example syllabus statements spanning the full spectrum: total ban with F-and-departmental-notation (IFP writing program); disclose-via-form with conference-before-discipline (University Writing); brainstorming-only with disclosure (Sociology stats); permitted-with-APA-citation (Education doctoral); light-editing-only (SAIS economics); doctoral "supplement" autonomy.
7. Course-data research requires IRB application.

## Sophistication markers
- FERPA de-identification depth and re-identification realism match the best formal policies; the syllabus-statement corpus openly displays intra-university divergence as a feature.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured** — faculty-facing guidance with real regulatory teeth (FERPA/HIPAA/IP) and a living syllabus library.
