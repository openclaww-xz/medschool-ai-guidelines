# KCU — Medicine, reimagined: KCU equips future physicians (blog, Aug 2025)

## Authority / Authorship
- KCU marketing/news (Haley Reardon); quotes Joseph Williams (EdD, director of COM assessment & analytics).

## Clause inventory
1. Philosophy: "medicine is — and must be — a human profession. AI will never replace clinical judgment, but it can amplify it."
2. "Ethical, safe and equitable use of AI depends on informed clinicians... ensure students leave with not only technical fluency but a strong voice in how these tools are applied to serve all patients—not just the ones in the data."
3. Curriculum facts: comprehensive AI training from year one (ML/data-analytics building blocks; prompting; computer vision; language generation; capstone); data-analysis module; case debates on privacy/fairness/equity.
4. POCUS: Butterfly Network ScanLab partnership fully integrated 2025-26 ("AI to guide probe placement, label anatomy and grade scan quality in real time"); eFAST/lung/cardiac certifications.

## Classification
- **not-a-policy (curriculum-investment story)** — KCU's AI posture is curricular, not regulatory (consistent with the library's no-policy disclosure); the AI-from-year-one + AI-guided POCUS integration is a pioneer-grade *curriculum* story with zero learner-conduct rules.
