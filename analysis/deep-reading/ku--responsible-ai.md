# KU — Responsible AI

## Authority / Authorship
- University of Kansas (ai.ku.edu); governance bodies: concluded KU AI Taskforce (2023-25), KU AI & Privacy Risk Council (NIST AI RMF-informed), KUMC AI Steering Committee (separate capture).

## Clause inventory
1. Seven guiding principles: Human Centeredness ("people stay in charge"); Value Alignment; Equitability (bias mitigation, "biased results should not be relied upon"); Privacy/Security/Safety ("use approved tools"); Accountability; Transparency; Validity ("evidence based, dependable").
2. "The use of artificial intelligence at KU and KUMC is governed by existing university policies, many of which are technology neutral" — enumerated: data privacy, security, research integrity, human subjects, academic conduct, accessibility, records, procurement, acceptable use.
3. Risk Council scope: assess privacy/security/compliance risks; advise on high-risk/novel applications; does "not approve individual research projects or replace existing governance bodies."

## Classification
- **cautious-informed (governance hub)** — principle layer over technology-neutral existing policy, with a NIST-anchored risk council; substantive rules stay at KUMC level.
