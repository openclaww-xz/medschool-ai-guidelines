# Deep Read — LSUHSC-NO Policy on Use of Generative AI by Medical Students

## Authority / Authorship
- Office of the Dean, School of Medicine. Last Review/Revision/Approval: October 2025; Review Cycle: Annual.
- **Four-body approval chain**: Preclerkship Course Director Committee, Clerkship Director Committee, Curriculum Renewal Committee, Curriculum Steering Committee. Responsible Dean: Associate Dean for Undergraduate Medical Education. "Relevant LCME Element: n/a."
- References cite McGovern (UTHealth), Northwestern Feinberg, UCSF Bridges, UVA SOM, UAB — explicit borrowing from early-adopter policies (both fellow cohort-C members and others).
- **AI-assisted drafting disclosure**: "Portions of this policy were developed with the assistance of OpenAI's ChatGPT, a large language model. Content generated was reviewed, edited, and approved by LSUSHC-NO faculty and policy stakeholders." (Unique meta-transparency.)

## Philosophy (verbatim framing quotes)
- "This policy aims to promote responsible, ethical, and effective integration of AI technologies in medical education, ensuring alignment with LSUHSC-NO values of academic integrity, professionalism, and patient safety."
- "Misuse and over-reliance on generative AI tools can lead to misinformation, misunderstanding, and harm."
- "Students must never use generative AI as a substitute for their own knowledge acquisition, analysis or self-reflection."

## Complete Verbatim Clause Inventory

### Definitions
- Generative AI Tools: "Software applications that produce text, images, or other content based on user inputs (including, but not limited to, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, DALL-E)."
- Confidential institutional data: information "not intended for public disclosure... any data protected under HIPAA (such as patient health information), FERPA (such as student educational records), intellectual property, research data, as well as any other information that could identify individuals, reveal proprietary research, or expose internal operations, clinical, financial, or administrator details."
- External cloud services: third-party platforms "not approved or managed by LSUHSC-NO IT Department."

### General Principles
- **Academic Integrity**: "Unauthorized use of AI tools to generate assignments or assessments constitutes academic misconduct" under Honor Code; "Students and Faculty are responsible for the content they produce, regardless of AI assistance"; scholarly submissions must follow journal AI policies "and include appropriate disclosure of AI use."
- **Transparency**: "Where given explicit permission to engage in use of AI tools, students must disclose their use, including the specific tool used and the extent of its contribution to the assignment."
- **Privacy and Confidentiality** (capitalized NEVER): "Students must NEVER input any protected health information (PHI) or confidential institutional data in any AI tools or external cloud services." Use "must be done under the context of institutional credentials (IE. LSUHSC-NO, LCMCHealth, FMOL, etc)."
- **Security**: PHI storage/transmission prohibited in "AI tools, external cloud services, browser plug-ins, third-party integrations, or consumer applications (EverNote, REmarkable, etc.) unless those services have been formally vetted and approved by LSUHSC-NO's Information Security Office." **Named licensed stack**: "LSUHSC-NO currently has formal licensing for Microsoft (including OneDrive, Teams, and Copilot Chat), Zoom, and Adobe Firefly. These tools should be used with LSUHSC-NO credentials to ensure that privacy agreements apply."
- **Faculty Discretion**: faculty "have the authority and are encouraged to set and discuss specific guidelines"; "Unless otherwise stated by the faculty member, upload of any course materials is not permitted."
- **Continued Education**: training encouraged.
- **Responsible Use**: over-reliance warning (above).

### Phase-differentiated Guidelines (two-column table)
**Preclerkship (L1/L2)**:
- Permissible: "summarizing or clarifying complex topics, generating study questions, brainstorming"; encouraged to consult faculty on tools and "verify the information is correct with cross-referencing credible sources."
- Prohibited: "Using AI tools at any time for or during assessments such as tests and quizzes, using AI tools for assignments unless explicitly allowed, submitting AI-generated content as original work without proper citation and attribution, relying solely on AI generated content without independent evaluation or understanding."
- "students must never upload curricular materials (lecture slides, learning guides, practice exam questions, etc.) into AI systems which are not protected or sanctioned by LSUHSC-NO IT, and then only with explicit permission from the responsible Faculty."
**Clerkship (L3/4)**:
- Permissible: summarizing complex topics; "using for general medical knowledge enhancement such as literature review and summarization, guideline review"; "brainstorming differential diagnoses and treatment plans"; "using hospital/institution-approved AI functionalities integrated within electronic health record system."
- Prohibited: "Inputting any PHI into non-approved AI platforms"; "generating clinical documentation (ex. H&P, progress note, discharge summary) using external AI tools that are not hospital approved"; "using AI tools to make clinical decisions without appropriate supervision and validation"; assessments; assignments unless allowed; uncited AI content.

### Enforcement
- "Violations of this policy will be addressed in accordance with LSUHSC-NO Honor Code and relevant disciplinary procedures."

## Sophistication Markers
- **Phase-differentiated (preclerkship vs clerkship) permissible/prohibited matrix** — the clearest curricular-stage differentiation in the corpus; clerkship column distinguishes EHR-integrated AI (permitted) from external tools (prohibited).
- Named enterprise license stack incl. Copilot Chat and Adobe Firefly.
- Curricular-material upload protection (IP/prospect-theft rationale — practice exam questions).
- Cited source policies + ChatGPT-assisted drafting disclosure (meta-accountability).

## Distinctives
- Only policy in cohort that openly discloses its own AI-assisted drafting.
- "NEVER input" register (caps) — strong deontic language.
- Four-committee approval chain reflects curriculum-governance integration unusual for so short a policy.
