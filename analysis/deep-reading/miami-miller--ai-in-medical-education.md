2. Vision: "a precision medical education ecosystem where evidence, analytics, and AI-enabled tools personalize learning; where assessment is trustworthy and competency based."
3. Guiding principles: Safety/Equity/Accountability ("require transparency in all AI tools and models, and maintain thorough documentation"); Academic Integrity & Professionalism ("comply with the Miller School's AI policies, with clear expectations for disclosure"); Pedagogy ("evidence-based strategies rooted in educational theory"); Data Stewardship (FERPA/HIPAA/IRB; "privacy by design"); "Human in and on the Loop."
