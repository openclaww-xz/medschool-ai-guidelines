# Mount Sinai — AI Governance and Safety (web page)

## Authority / authorship
- Icahn School of Medicine / MSHS institutional page describing the governance structure that operates MSHS Policy 223. Authored by/for **DTP (Digital and Technology Partners)** — "DTP's Technology and Enablement Governance provides essential guidance on the technology needs that support AI across Mount Sinai."
- Committee structure: **AI Executive Committee** (reports to Strategy Group and Clinical Chairs; "oversees all others"), **AI Risks, Ethics, and Policy Committee**, **AI Teaching, Learning, Discovery, and Research Committee**, **AI Review Board**.

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- Guiding principles: "keeping AI safe, effective, responsible, secure, and ethical, while prioritizing organizational goals, regulatory compliance, and risk mitigation."
- Lifecycle framing: "Effective AI lifecycle management ensures that clinical and workforce productivity AI initiatives are safe, effective, responsible, secure, and strategically aligned with health system objectives."
- Marketing-communications transparency stance: "we also believe that transparency about our use of AI to our patients, readers, and stakeholders is key to building trust internally and externally."

## Complete verbatim clause inventory
**Governance structure:**
1. "Our AI governance structure establishes policies and standards for the ethical and effective use of artificial intelligence throughout the Health System."
2. "AI Executive Committee – Provides strategic guidance, determines AI investment strategy, and reports to the Strategy Group and Clinical Chairs committees. This committee oversees all others."
3. "AI Risks, Ethics, and Policy Committee – Focuses on AI ethics and policy development, proactively identifying risks and facilitating progress across the following functional AI committees:"
4. "AI Teaching, Learning, Discovery, and Research Committee – Explores AI's potential to enhance teaching and learning throughout the Health System."
5. "AI Review Board – Works to integrate and optimize AI in clinical care and workforce productivity by reviewing AI requests, ensuring selected projects follow appropriate processes, and promoting AI awareness and education throughout the Health System."
6. "As a foundation to all of the above, DTP's Technology and Enablement Governance provides essential guidance on the technology needs that support AI across Mount Sinai."

**DTP AI Governance Team role:**
7. "advances system-wide clinical and operational AI priorities through standardized rules, guidelines, processes, and requirements that shape how AI is designed, procured, implemented and deployed across the Health System."
8. "Manage the AI lifecycle to ensure that clinical and operational workforce productivity AI initiatives are safe, effective, responsible, secure, and aligned with Health System objectives."
9. "Manage AI lifecycle by evaluating solutions, supporting small pilots, and implementing checkpoints to measure success. These steps ensure a structured approach before scaling AI solutions enterprise-wide."
10. "Oversee progress, mitigate risks, and drive value realization while fostering transparency, innovation, and a collaborative culture to advance AI capabilities and accelerate impactful AI adoption."

**Marketing/Communications AI rules:**
11. "At no point will any material generated, altered, or enhanced by AI be published or go live without human oversight to check for accuracy, plagiarism, or biases. This applies to all use cases listed below."
12. "We do not use information generated by AI tools without checking it with relevant experts, resources, and stakeholders for accuracy."
13. "We do not enter any sensitive information, including patient information or confidential information, into any AI platform that has not been vetted and approved by the Health System."
14. Permitted uses: "We might use generative AI to create generic visual assets, including background textures, single objects, music and sounds, data visualizations and graphs, illustrations, and animations."
15. IP guardrails: "we ensure we do not infringe any trademarks or copyrighted material, violate or infringe on the terms of any licensed content, and our use is in accordance with any applicable Fair Use and Creative Commons license."
16. Publicity rights: "we do not create images about individuals without their consent."
17. "We might use AI to help optimize copy to make it effective in reaching a targeted audience."
18. "We might use generative AI to brainstorm story ideas or speaking points, and create summaries of complex/lengthy subjects."

**AI Ideas intake:**
19. "Do you have an idea for an artificial intelligence product or solution? We welcome you to submit your idea using the AI Idea Intake Form, available in Service Now. Your proposal will be reviewed, evaluated, and prioritized by the appropriate members of our AI governance structure."

**AI Portfolio:**
20. "DTP provides a centralized list of all clinical, operational, and financial AI models and features, which includes both predictive and generative technologies. This central portfolio helps increase awareness of existing AI tools, thereby reducing duplication and enhancing efficiency, consistency, and collaboration across the Mount Sinai Health System."

## Sophistication markers
- **Clinical deployment governance:** confirms the MSHS 223 machinery is real and operating — intake form in ServiceNow, committee routing, portfolio registry. "Evaluating solutions, supporting small pilots, and implementing checkpoints to measure success" = pilot-before-scale pipeline.
- **Monitoring:** "implementing checkpoints to measure success," "closely monitoring progress, assessing potential risks, and measuring value realization" — plus the quarterly×24-month review cadence in the underlying policy. Among the best-evidenced monitoring regimes in the corpus.
- **Model-failure handling:** not detailed on this page (see policy reporting procedures).
- **Ambient-scribe consent:** not on this page.
- **Clinician liability:** not addressed.
- Portfolio-registry practice (anti-duplication, enterprise inventory of all AI models) is an operations-grade marker few systems publish.

## Distinctives
- Published AI-use rules for marketing/communications — a niche governance surface almost no other system addresses publicly.
- ServiceNow idea-intake with prioritization by governance committees — open innovation funnel.
- Explicit pilot→checkpoint→scale sequencing language.

## Provisional classification
**Pioneer.** Together with MSHS Policy 223, this shows governance + tooling (registry, intake, portfolio) + staged monitoring — the three-leg test for deployment pioneer. The page itself is descriptive PR-adjacent, but it documents operating machinery, not aspirations.
