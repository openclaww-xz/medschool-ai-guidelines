# MSU — Generative AI: Student Guidance

## Authority / Authorship
- MSU (ethics.msu.edu/gen-ai/student).

## Clause inventory
1. "Please follow the policies provided by your instructors or course syllabuses before using AI on assignments."
2. Principles: Intentional Integration ("a tool for idea generation, brainstorming or drafting, not as a substitute for active learning"); People-oriented Approach; Transparency & Disclosure ("Communicate clearly and specifically... if/when you use AI"); Think Critically (hallucination literacy); Stay Informed.
3. Sourcing/ethics: "Never assume that a generative AI tool is 'safe' or 'private'... unless you are expressly told so by MSU IT."
4. "Never enter the information or intellectual property... of another person into a generative AI tool, unless you have their expressed written consent."
5. Interim Guidance on Data Uses and Risks referenced; group-project consent norm ("always inform your classmates and confirm they agree").

## Classification
- **progressive-structured** — the third-party-IP written-consent clause and group-consent norm are unusual, humane additions to a standard ask-first scaffold.
