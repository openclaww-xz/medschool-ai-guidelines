# MUSC — AI Acceptable Use Framework for Academic Tasks (framework page)

*Framework PDF deep-read in run 29; this page adds implementation narrative.*

## Authority / Authorship
- MUSC Center for the Advancement of Teaching and Learning (Julaine Fowlin, Exec. Dir.; E'lise Nissen, Dir. of AI in Education and Scholarship); provost-level sponsorship across three provosts; chief AI officer Marylyn Ritchie.

## Additional substance beyond the PDF
1. Philosophy: "moves the conversation beyond cheating and toward AI literacy, transparency and trust"; "a centralized way of communicating a mindset and a philosophy rather than telling faculty what to do."
2. The five categories (No AI / AI planning / AI limited / AI extensive / AI exploration) with worked examples; "Every category except the first could potentially require documentation."
3. Provenance: "initially informed by the Artificial Intelligence Assessment Scale, published in the Journal of University Teaching and Learning Practice in 2024... further adapted using the updated AI Assessment Scale."
4. Ecosystem: required "AI at MUSC" course for all new students; employee AI training; Honor Code orientation video (full transcript captured) with centralized student-run Honor Council; failure-to-report is itself a violation.
5. Student-trust rationale: "many students do not trust higher education to guide them in when, how and why to use AI"; faculty encouraged to offer alternatives for AI-averse students.
6. National diffusion: webinars, OneHE interview, Furze blog, other institutions mirroring.

## Classification
- **pioneer (implementation layer)** — the framework page demonstrates the full institutional surround (course, council, training, alternatives, diffusion) that turns a scale into a culture.
