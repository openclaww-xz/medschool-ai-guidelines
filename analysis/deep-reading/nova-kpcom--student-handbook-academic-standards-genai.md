# NSU KPCOM — Student Handbook 2025-26: Academic Standards (GenAI provision)

## Authority / Authorship
- KPCOM Student Handbook 2025-26 (p.32 academic-standards section), nested in the original-work standard.

## Clause inventory (complete GenAI provision)
1. "Students' use of generative artificial intelligence (e.g., ChatGPT, Google Bard, DALL-E, Midjourney, etc.) or similar resources on any coursework or academic assessments without the prior permission of their faculty member, or the use of these resources in any way that violates the academic standards of NSU and/or a student's academic program, is expressly prohibited."
2. Surrounding integrity architecture: original-work definition; referencing standards; tendering-of-information prohibition (incl. posting exam content online); enumerated violations (plagiarism through bribery/forgery).

## Classification
- **conservative-naive** — single prohibition-default clause naming 2023-era tools (Bard, DALL-E, Midjourney); no disclosure format, PHI clause, or tools; pure containment within a standard integrity code.
