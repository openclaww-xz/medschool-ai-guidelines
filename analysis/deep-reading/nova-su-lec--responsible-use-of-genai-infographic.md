# NSU LEC — Responsible Use of Generative AI (F.A.S.T.E.R. infographic, 2024)

## Authority / Authorship
- NSU Learning and Educational Center, Oct 2024; adapts the Government of Canada "Guide on the Use of Generative AI."

## Clause inventory (F.A.S.T.E.R.)
- **F**air (bias, human-rights compliance); **A**ccountable ("Take responsibility for the content generated... factual, legal, ethical"); **S**ecure (match security classification; protect personal info); **T**ransparent ("Identify content that has been produced using GAI; notify users that they are interacting with an AI tool"); **E**ducated (strengths/limitations, prompting); **R**elevant ("AI tools aren't the best choice in every situation").

## Classification
- **cautious-informed (one-page infographic)** — governmental-framework adaptation; the entire normative layer for NSU students outside the KPCOM prohibition.
