# OSU — Responsible Use (ai.osu.edu; student GenAI rules)

## Authority / Authorship
- Ohio State AI initiative (ai.osu.edu); student/instructor/researcher/staff question sets.

## Clause inventory
1. **Core rule**: "Students should use GenAI tools for coursework only with the explicit permission of each instructor, in the ways allowed by that instructor."
2. Student self-audit question sets: Learning & Growth ("Am I using this... to bypass the learning process?"); Integrity; Accuracy ("Have I checked for hallucinations?"); Equity ("Does my use of GenAI give me an unfair advantage?"); Ethics.
3. Instructor sets: learning-outcome impact; "Should I disclose when I use AI to generate materials or feedback?"; assignment redesign against AI; "Do I understand the limitations of detection tools and the due process for misconduct?"; FERPA check.
4. Researcher sets: purpose-fit; transparency; bias; ownership.
5. Strengths/weaknesses catalog (proofreading, brainstorming, code, visuals... vs hallucinations, bias, privacy, citation, cultural limits, outdated knowledge, "Lack of critical thinking or judgment").
6. Tool-comparison guidance (vendor data-use differences).
7. Links: Academic Affairs guidance; marketing-communications guidelines; OTDI security statement.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured (reflective-practice layer)** — Socratic question-based guidance rather than rules; permission-explicit student default; the introspection format is the distinctive.
