## Authority / Authorship
- PSOM Office of Organization Effectiveness; staff-development orientation (workshops grounded in Knowles' andragogy, Kolb, Merrill); routes to Penn ISC AI Guidance, Penn Medicine AI Hub, PMACS.

## Clause inventory
1. Hub framing: "PSOM is integrating AI to accelerate discovery, improve medical education, streamline administrative activities, and enhance clinical care."
2. Do's & Don'ts: approved tools; verify accuracy; transparency; protect data vs. sensitive info in unapproved tools; no AI without human judgment; review and attribute AI content.
3. C.R.A.F.T. prompt model + role-based Copilot Prompt Library for PSOM workflows.
4. Copilot Chat access (replaces Penn AI Chat): "It's a violation of HIPAA and Penn Medicine policy to share patient or proprietary Penn Medicine data on public AI tools"; approved for "Low, Moderate, and most High Risk Data"; no SSNs/credit cards.
5. Research: PMACS Service Information Officers advise on AI analysis of de-identified data, cloud vs local models, AI Committee/IRB triggers.
6. Workshop/prompt-library/course ecosystem.
