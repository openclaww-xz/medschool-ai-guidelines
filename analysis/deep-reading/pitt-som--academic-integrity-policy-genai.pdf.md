# UPSOM — Policy on Academic Integrity (GenAI provisions)

## Authority / Authorship
- University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine, Office of Medical Education; Curriculum Committee approved March 24, 2026. LCME 3.5 cited.

## Clause inventory
1. Eleven enumerated integrity violations; #7: presenting "content derived from generative artificial intelligence (AI) technologies, without customary and proper acknowledgment of sources."
2. Procedures section: "The use of generative artificial intelligence may be permissible, as long as it is appropriately cited or integrated within the expectations of a course/faculty. A course director, for instance, could ask a student to use an AI platform as part of an assignment."
3. "Absent attribution or explicit/concrete permissions, however, use of AI-generated content on any assessment activity (test, paper, other required assignment) would be considered academic dishonesty."
4. "Course/clerkship syllabi should explicitly outline permissions/restrictions related to AI usage for that curricular unit."
5. Due process via Student Progress & Promotions Committee / Professionalism Subcommittee; sanctions from restorative justice to dismissal.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — clean 2026 committee-approved integration of GenAI into an existing integrity policy; permission+citation default, no tools/tiers/clinical clauses.
