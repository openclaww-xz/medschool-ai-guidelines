Institution-facing priorities:
1. "Select—and equitably provide—high-quality, ethical GenAI tools across the University community."
2. "Develop policy regarding use of GenAI in 'sensitive' contexts involving hiring and evaluation of personnel and admission and evaluation of students."
3. "Foster an AI-literate campus culture by providing incentivized education... for faculty, staff, and students."
4. "Integrate AI tools as appropriate to promote a student-centered educational culture."
