# Rush CTEI — Your Classroom Faculty Guide to AI

## Authority / Authorship
- Rush University Center for Teaching Excellence and Innovation (faculty-development unit).

## Clause inventory
1. Definitional section (AI/GenAI; transformer vs diffusion).
2. Best practices: pedagogical purpose first; learner-centered; ongoing professional development.
3. Usage & citation: set expectations; fact-check culture; "Require your students to declare their use of AI sources with an AI Use Disclosure... even if they only used AI tools for brainstorming"; full APA and MLA citation formats for AI worked out.
4. AI-vulnerability redesign strategies: process artifacts (notebooks, outlines); "Determine whether an essay is needed"; higher-order-thinking focus; reference-specific requirements incl. screenshots of key passages.
5. **AI-detection analysis**: false-negative/false-positive mechanics; "perplexity"/"burstiness" explained; GPTZero-flagged-US-Constitution anecdote; OpenAI withdrawing its detector; "not advisable for instructors to rely on AI detectors, including Turnitin's, as the sole means."
6. Assessment redesign: "design assignments that ensure authentic assessments that mitigate (or incorporate) the use of AI."
7. Leaning-in section (personalized learning, automated grading, research acceleration) with ethics caveats.
