# Rutgers IT — Guidance on the use of AI

## Authority / Authorship
- Rutgers OIT; anchored in Academic Integrity Policy 10.2.13 and Information Classification Policy 70.1.2.

## Clause inventory
1. "Only Rutgers-approved AI tools should be used at the university"; licensed tools assure "your data will not be used to train the models."
2. Academic integrity: AI "should not be considered permissible for coursework unless clearly stated or communicated by instructors"; quoted policy language: coursework must be "the student's own and created without the aid of impermissible technologies, materials, or collaborations."
3. Confidential/PHI/proprietary data restrictions per classification chart.
4. Review-and-verify duty (bias, inaccuracy, unattributed copyright).
5. Purchases follow standard security risk assessment + digital accessibility standards.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — tidy IT guidance marrying an enacted integrity policy to a tool whitelist; no health-sciences-specific content.
