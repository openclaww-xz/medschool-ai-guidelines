## Complete AI-relevant clause inventory
1. Integrity practice #2: "Acknowledging the source of information whether taken from another person, artificial intelligence, or other technology."
2. Cheating definition: unauthorized "artificial intelligence or other technology, cell phones... not authorized by the instructor."
3. Plagiarism definition (4 sub-clauses): presenting work generated "by artificial intelligence, or by other technology without... proper citation"; paraphrasing AI-generated ideas without citation; presenting AI output "so that it appears to be the student's own work"; submitting work "prepared, either entirely or in part, by... artificial intelligence... without proper citation."
4. Faculty duties: syllabus "explicit statement on use of artificial intelligence and/or other technology"; assignment design to minimize dishonesty.
5. Adjudication machinery: Faculty-Guided Resolution; Academic Hearing Panels; preponderance standard; concealment (failure-to-report) as separate violation; retaliation ban; appeal to Provost.
