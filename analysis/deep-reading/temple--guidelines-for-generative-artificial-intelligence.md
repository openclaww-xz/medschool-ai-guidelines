# Temple — Guidelines for Generative AI (President's Office, July 2024)

## Authority / Authorship
- Issuing Authority: Office of the President; Responsible Officer: VP for Information Technology; Effective July 24, 2024.

## Complete clause inventory
1. Pre-approval: "All software purchased or accessed must go through the Pre-Approval Purchasing Process."
2. Confidentiality: never input PII/PHI/FERPA/export-control data into public GenAI; "incognito features in certain AI applications do not guarantee confidentiality"; non-public data requires CISO pre-approval.
3. Copyright/IP: review outputs for plagiarism; "your intellectual property, if uploaded to a GenAI application, may no longer be protected and private."
4. Accuracy review duty.
5. **Equity clause**: "the cost of certain AI applications can be prohibitive for some individuals. Any use of GenAI should consider the impact on the source and target audience."
6. Transparency: cite properly; "be sure participants are all aware and consent to its use."
7. Enumerated never-use data (NPI, PHI, PII, patented inventions).
8. **AI-notetaker ban**: "All other third-party AI note-takers (such as Reader.AI or Otter.AI) and any other third-party proxy are prohibited in all Temple-owned Zoom sessions, Microsoft Teams meetings..." (Zoom AI Companion host-controlled exception).

## Classification
- **cautious-informed (university guidelines)** — data-security core with three standout clauses: consent-to-AI-use, cost-equity, and third-party notetaker prohibition.
