# Temple Katz — AI Task Force launch announcement

## Authority / Authorship
- Lewis Katz School of Medicine; tri-led by Akiyah Baugh (IT Director), Ryan Gibbons (Asst. Dean Faculty Affairs), Munir Gunes Kutlu (Neural Sciences); endorsed by Dean Amy J. Goldberg.

## Clause inventory
1. Mandate: "develop school-wide standards for responsible AI use, including guidance on data governance, privacy, risk mitigation, ethical principles, experimentation, evaluation, and deployment"; evaluate applications for strategic fit; "pilot-test applications of interest."
2. Six subcommittees: Curriculum/Education & Faculty AI Training; Clinical Care; Research; Operational Efficiency; Compliance/Risk/Ethics; Infrastructure/Resources/Training/Communication.
3. Alignment with University and Temple Health AI task forces ("coordinated, enterprise-level approach").
4. Katz Portal AI resource section forthcoming.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed (task-force formation)** — the announcement itself contains no learner rules; significance is the six-subcommittee blueprint mirroring KUMC's composition model. Policy presumably in development as of capture.
