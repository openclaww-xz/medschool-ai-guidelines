# Deep Read — TTUHSC AI Use (compliance page)

## Authority / Authorship
- Office of Institutional Compliance web page — "the official source for TTUHSC AI governance, policies, guidance, training."
- Governance: **AI Governance and Risk Committee, established by the President's Cabinet** — "approves AI policies, reviews and assesses AI tools and associated risks, monitors performance and compliance, evaluates vendors, oversees training, and addresses reported concerns."
- Formal policy instrument referenced: **TTUHSC OP 52.21 – Acceptable Use of AI Tools** (content behind accordion headings, not expanded in capture: patient-info entry, research use, tool approval status, accidental disclosure, trustworthiness, calculators/Word scope). Data classification: OP 56.04 (PDF gated: "CONTACT US FOR PDF").
- **Texas statutory driver**: AI Literacy Training "certified by the Texas Department of Information Resources (DIR) and satisfies the State of Texas AI literacy training requirements" (delivered via ACME, eRaider login, "Responsible AI Use" course + quiz).

## Philosophy (verbatim framing quotes)
- "TTUHSC is committed to ensuring AI is used responsibly, ethically, and in compliance with institutional and regulatory requirements."
- "AI is a tool to support your work, not replace professional judgment."
- Guidance goals: "Promote responsible and ethical AI use; Protect personal, confidential, and regulated information; Ensure compliance with institutional policies and legal requirements; Support innovation while minimizing risk."

## Complete Verbatim Clause Inventory

### Allowable Use
- "AI tools may be used when appropriate and in compliance with institutional policy. Publicly available information may be entered into AI tools, provided its use aligns with applicable guidelines and data governance requirements."

### Prohibited Use
- "Using unapproved AI tools with sensitive or regulated data" (TTUHSC Data Classifications OP 56.04).
- "Entering confidential information into unapproved AI systems."
- "Generating non-public or restricted content such as: Research data; Legal analysis; **Grading decisions**; Personnel decisions."
- "Engaging in fraudulent, illegal, or policy-violating activities."

### Governance posture
- **No approved tools yet**: "Approved AI tools that meet institutional privacy and security standards are currently under evaluation. Until formal approval is confirmed, AI tools must not be used with protected or confidential information without prior review." (interim-lockdown posture)
- Privacy commitment "to protecting the privacy of patients, learners, and team members."

### Policy FAQ topics (headings only, content not captured)
"Can I enter patient information into an AI tool?"; "Is it acceptable to use AI for research activities?"; "How do I know if an AI tool is approved?"; "What should I do if I accidentally enter sensitive information into an unapproved AI tool?"; "Can AI-generated information be trusted?"; "Are calculators, Word, or basic software considered AI tools?"

### Training
- Literacy training for all team members: AI overview, responsible use, policies, ethics, privacy/security, "Texas state requirements related to AI."

### Incident contact
- Report potential incidents to Office of Institutional Compliance (phone/email); named individual contact (shen.wang@ttuhsc.edu).
