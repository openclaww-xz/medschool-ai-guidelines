# Tufts TTS — Guidance for Using AI at Tufts

## Authority / Authorship
- Tufts Technology Services; complemented by OVPE Generative AI Usage Guidelines; AI Council / AI Task Force structure.

## Complete clause inventory
1. Tool categories by risk (text-gen, media-gen, embedded enterprise AI, discipline-specific).
2. Approved-tools regime (reviewed for privacy, security, accessibility, contractual terms); unapproved tools require review — esp. institutional data.
3. **Development approval**: building/fine-tuning/deploying AI (custom apps/agents, training on Tufts data, decision-affecting deployment) requires TTS consult + review processes.
4. Accountability: "You are responsible for all work, decisions, and outputs that involve AI, regardless of the tool used."
5. Transparency expectations (disclose when AI materially contributes; "When in doubt, disclose").
6. Limitations literacy (fabrication, bias, confident-when-wrong, behavior drift).
7. **Vendor-T&C literacy section** — the deepest in the corpus: training/fine-tuning on user inputs; retention and human review of prompts; broad output licenses conflicting with publishing/IP/sponsored research; use-case restrictions (clinical contexts!); disclaimer of warranties; click-through prohibition without authority; personal-subscription prohibition.
8. Data handling: FERPA/HIPAA/human-subjects/HR/legal-sensitive/contract-restricted content banned absent approval; "Assume that information entered into unapproved or public AI tools is no longer private."

## Classification
- **progressive-structured (IT layer)** — the vendor-terms clause set (esp. clinical use-case restrictions in licenses) is guidance-layer best practice.
