# Tulane IT — IT Guide for Generative AI Platforms

## Authority / Authorship
- Tulane Provost's Office + IT joint guidance; governance via IT Governance Committee + OGC vendor terms.

## Clause inventory
1. Consult ILC/CELT for teaching uses.
2. "Do not use generative AI tools for high-risk activities (e.g., hiring, student assessments, or legal matters) without first consulting with the Tulane IT and Information Security."
3. Medium/High-risk info ban in public tools (enumerated incl. "confidential information from research partners").
4. Accuracy-verification duty.
5. "Be Transparent About Your Use of AI Tools... you must clearly state how AI was used" in research/educational outputs.
6. Tool-comparison blurbs (Copilot enterprise-grade; ChatGPT; Claude; Gemini; Meta AI; Perplexity; Grok) — informal but current.
7. Workshops (Copilot, BoodleBox).

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — compact IT guidance; high-risk-activity consult gate and vendor-term governance are the substance.
