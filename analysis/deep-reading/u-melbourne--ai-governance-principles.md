# University of Melbourne — AI at Melbourne: AI governance and AI Principles

## (a) Authority and authorship
- Publisher: University of Melbourne (institution-wide, above faculty level — covers education, research, workforce/operations).
- Governance organ named: the **Generative Artificial Intelligence Taskforce (GAIT)**, "Chaired by the Deputy Vice-Chancellor (Academic)"; members include the DVC (People and Community), Chief Operating Officer, Chief Information Officer, Academic Registrar, plus research portfolio, enterprise performance group and organisational development representatives. Whole-of-university executive governance.
- The actual "University of Melbourne AI principles" document is linked but its text is not reproduced on this page — the corpus captures the governance wrapper, not the principles text.

## (b) Policy philosophy
- Posture: coordinated institutional risk-and-opportunity management. Verbatim: "The University's response to the risks and opportunities associated with generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) tools and systems is **overseen by** the Generative Artificial Intelligence Taskforce (GAIT)." And: "GenAI has the potential to **impact across all parts of the University's Strategy**."
- Principles' stated function: "intended to **support decision-making** in relation to AI tools and technologies across the scope of the University's activities and **help guide actions** around their adoption and use" — decision-support governance, not rule enforcement.

## (c) Complete clause inventory
1. Oversight clause: GAIT oversees the University's response to GenAI risks and opportunities (verbatim in (b)).
2. Scope clause: GenAI impacts "all parts of the University's Strategy"; GAIT "coordinates activities across **three main focus areas: Education, Research and Workforce and Operations**."
3. Composition clause: chair + membership as listed in (a) — a named, senior, cross-functional executive body.
4. Principles clause: AI Principles "[developed] by GAIT... support decision-making... help guide actions around their adoption and use" (verbatim in (b)).
5. Contact/remediation clause: "For additional information on GAIT's work, to **raise a GenAI related issue, or seek advice** please contact the Taskforce." — a standing escalation channel.
- No binding rules, sanctions, or AI-specific prohibitions on this page; it establishes governance architecture only.

## (d) Sophistication markers
- Marker: standing taskforce with CIO/COO/DVC membership — AI governance seated at executive level with an operational escalation path ("raise a GenAI related issue"), not just a policy library.
- Technical content: none on this page (generic "tools and systems" language).

## (e) Distinctives vs sibling frameworks
- Among university documents, Melbourne is the only one in the corpus naming a permanent executive taskforce spanning education + research + workforce/operations; siblings (UvA, UofT) have committees but Melbourne publishes the sharpest organ-and-channel description.
- Explicitly frames AI as strategic (all-of-strategy), not academic-integrity-only.

## (f) Provisional classification
**progressive-structured** — institutionalized, multi-portfolio governance with decision-support principles; permissive adoption posture; no enforcement or technical specificity on this page (the substance sits behind the un-captured principles PDF).
