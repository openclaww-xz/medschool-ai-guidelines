# URMC Miner Library — AI: Ethics and Policy LibGuide

## Authority / Authorship
- University of Rochester Medical Center Miner Library.

## Clause inventory
1. LLM ethics: "Researchers should disclose their use of LLMs and indicate which parts of the text were written or co-written"; expert checking; disclose AI impact even without AI text; no fabrication via LLM.
2. Detection vs disclosure: "Detection is complicated, encouraging disclosure is recommended."
3. Disclosure schema (methods section AND references): AI model used; training data; "Potential bias and limitations"; "AI's role in the project (e.g., Info retrieval, data analysis, decision-making)."
4. AS&E classroom guide pointer (learning-goal alignment; alternative assignments for students declining to upload personal details/IP).
5. External refs: EO 14110, OMB memo, OpenAI privacy center/bug bounty.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured (library layer)** — the disclosure-schema (role-in-project) is richer than most policies demand; consistent with Rochester's provost guidance stack (separate notes).
