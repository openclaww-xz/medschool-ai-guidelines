# U Rochester Provost — Generative AI Use in Education

## Authority / Authorship
- Provost's education subcommittee of the University AI Council (15 named members incl. SMD Vice Dean of Education Sarah Peyre, students, librarians, WMET); versioned Dec 2 2024 → Mar 7/May 16 2025 revisions.

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- "We promote a human-centered approach to GenAI implementation," rooted in "Meliora values of equity, leadership, integrity, openness, respect, and accountability."

## Complete clause inventory (principles)
1. Student Learning primacy; 2. Academic Freedom (instructor autonomy); 3. Accountability (human oversight); 4. Academic Integrity; 5. Bias vigilance ("trained on large, unmoderated datasets"); 6. Privacy & Security (no moderate/high-risk data into public tools); 7. Transparency (disclosure of AI-created work); 8. Equitable Access.
Instructor guidelines (10): learning-outcome-driven must/may/cannot decisions; disclose own AI teaching use; align course policy with school honesty policy; **per-assignment written policy** ("when students must, may, or cannot use GenAI"); equitable tool access incl. alternatives for objecting students; accuracy-education duty incl. "Students will not be held responsible for errors generated by the tool, provided they use it as directed"; GenAI-literacy instruction; grading human-oversight requirement; course-staff policies; **detection-tool caution** ("unreliable, biased, easily defeated, and unable to provide definitive evidence... avoid using... as the sole basis").
Student guidelines (7): learning-first ("workout partner at the gym" analogy); critical evaluation; privacy ("students should have the autonomy to decide whether to submit their own personal information"); **permission default: "If an instructor has not explicitly stated that GenAI is allowed for an assignment, assume it isn't"**; peer-educator restriction; honesty-policy alignment (plagiarism/cheating/falsification/collusion).
Implementation recommendations: centralized tool approval; funding for vetted subscriptions; literacy programs; resource hub; pilot programs.
**Self-disclosure**: "GenAI tools (ChatGPT, Claude) were used to edit drafts... for clarity and cohesion... the committee reviewed and revised this draft."

## Sophistication markers
- The tool-error responsibility carve-out (students not penalized for tool errors when used as directed) is unique in the corpus; evidence-cited (NIST AI 600-1, UNESCO, CHLOE-9); committee + AI-assisted drafting disclosed.

## Classification
- **pioneer (guidance tier)** — the most complete education-layer guidance document read this run: principled, evidence-cited, versioned, self-disclosing, with a distinctive learner-protection clause.
