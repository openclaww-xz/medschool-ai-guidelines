# U Rochester — Provost: Responsible Use of AI Tools and Institutional Data Security

## Authority / Authorship
- Signed email from Nicole Sampson, Provost & Chief Academic Officer, to the University community.

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- "It is imperative to remember that non-public or sensitive University information should never be uploaded into external AI tools—whether free or paid—unless there is a university agreement with the vendor approved by one of the various AI governance groups."

## Clause inventory
1. Never upload non-public/sensitive info to external AI tools absent a vendor agreement approved by "one of the various AI governance groups."
2. Secure chatbot provision: chat.rochester.edu — "a secure instance of OpenAI GPT customized for the University," handles medium/high-risk data, Duo 2FA off-network.
3. Help-desk contacts (Univ IT + URMC ISD).

## Sophistication markers
- Provisioned secure GPT instance; risk-tiered data language; dual IT help desks (university + health system).

## Distinctives
- The phrase "one of the various AI governance groups" is the governance layer made visible — multiple approving bodies already exist.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — provost-signed data-security guidance; correct, provisioned, but no academic/clinical-use content.
