# UAB — AI Usage (role-based guidance)

## Authority / Authorship
- UAB responsible-AI stack, role/context page.

## Complete clause inventory
1. Academics: faculty flexibility; CTL resources; "students benefit from clear and transparent expectations."
2. Research: transparency aligned with sponsor/publisher guidance; no unpublished drafts/data into public tools; "NIH prohibits the use of generative AI in its peer review process"; ICMJE transparency; validation duty.
3. Clinical: "Patient information and PHI must not be entered into public or unauthorized AI tools. HIPAA and UAB Health System policies always apply"; approved-tools-only innovation; "Human clinical judgment remains essential."
4. Administration: approved tools; non-sensitive info; IT/data/procurement/comms compliance; human review before use.
5. "When you are unsure, pause and ask."

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — compact four-context overlay; the clinical clauses are correct if unsurprising.
