# UC Davis Health — Clinical Note-Taking Technology (patient-facing Abridge page)

## Authority / authorship
- Patient-facing page under "Commitment to Quality and Safety" — communications/patient-experience authorship, describing a deployed vendor tool (Abridge ambient scribe).

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- "At UC Davis Health, we are always looking for ways to provide better care. That's why your clinician is using Abridge, a new technology that allows them to focus more on you and your health needs."

## Complete verbatim clause inventory
1. "This new service frees your clinician from data entry during your visit, meaning you get more focused attention from your clinician."
2. "Abridge records your conversation with your clinician during the visit. It then creates a summary using artificial intelligence (AI). After your visit, your clinician reviews the summary for accuracy, and adds it to your medical record."
3. "Abridge is HIPAA-compliant. All data is encrypted. Only your care team or authorized personnel have access to your information. All recordings are deleted after 30 days."
4. "The recording will be started, stopped, and paused by your clinician. They can pause the recording if you would rather that certain parts of the conversation not be recorded."
5. **Opt-out consent model:** "Do I have a choice about whether my clinician uses Abridge? Yes. If you do not want your clinician to use Abridge, just let them know. Your care will not be affected."
6. "Please speak with your clinician before, during, or after your visit. If you have additional concerns or complaints, contact UC Davis Health's Patient Relations Department..."
7. Multilingual Abridge info PDFs in 15 languages: Arabic, Chinese (simplified & traditional), Dari, Farsi, Hindi, Hmong, Korean, Pashto, Punjabi, Russian, Spanish, Tagalog, Ukrainian, Vietnamese.

## Sophistication markers
- **Ambient-scribe consent rules:** informal opt-out model — patient choice honored on request, "Your care will not be affected" (no-retaliation assurance), clinician-controlled start/stop/pause. NOT a documented per-encounter verbal consent (contrast UChicago: verbal consent every encounter + smartphrase documentation + declination charting). No mention of consent for third parties in the room.
- **Clinician review/attestation:** "your clinician reviews the summary for accuracy, and adds it to your medical record" — review-before-filing is stated; signing/attestation not detailed.
- **Monitoring/governance:** not on this page (see Analytics Oversight Committee page); data-retention limit (30-day recording deletion) and encryption are the privacy specifics.
- **Model-failure handling:** "reviews the summary for accuracy" is the only failure control.
- **Clinician liability:** not addressed; implied clinician responsibility for the record.

## Distinctives
- 15-language consent/education materials — the deepest language-access effort for ambient scribes in this corpus (Sacramento service-population driven).
- Concrete retention disclosure (recordings deleted after 30 days).

## Provisional classification
**Progressive-structured (deployment + consent surface).** Real deployed ambient scribe with an honest patient-facing explanation, language access, and an opt-out consent model; governance depth sits elsewhere. The opt-out (vs. opt-in documented consent) model is the main gap versus the residency-level gold standard.
