# UC Davis Health — Guidelines for Using Generative AI (web page)

## Authority / authorship
- **Compliance and Privacy Services** (regulatory/compliance office), with a named oversight body: the "**UC Davis Health Analytics Oversight Committee**." A fuller PDF exists ("Guidelines for Using Generative Artificial Intelligence (PDF)") but only the summary page was captured.

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- "Generative AI tools have the potential to be helpful, but UC Davis Health has restrictions on their use to protect patient privacy and data security."

## Complete verbatim clause inventory
1. "You should not be used generative AI tools with any patient information or other sensitive data unless the tool is specifically approved by the UC Davis Health." (sic — two typos in one clause: "You should not be used" and "the UC Davis Health")
2. "Even with approved tools, you should be careful about the information you provide and the output you receive, as generative AI may not be accurate and may reflect biases."
3. "As always consult with your supervisor and the UC Davis Health Analytics Oversight Committee before using a generative AI tool."
4. Anonymous reporting channels: "For anonymous reporting, please contact the UC Confidential Hotline at 1-800-403-4744 or report at the Whistleblower Hotline website."

## Sophistication markers
- **Clinical deployment governance:** minimal on the page — an Analytics Oversight Committee exists as the approval/reference body; no pipeline, risk tiers, or criteria visible.
- **Monitoring:** none.
- **Model-failure handling:** generic caution ("may not be accurate and may reflect biases").
- **Ambient-scribe consent:** absent.
- **Clinician liability:** none stated; supervisor consultation duty only.
- Whistleblower hotline as the escalation channel.
