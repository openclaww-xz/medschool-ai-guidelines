# UCSD — Privacy Guidelines for Uses of AI Assistants (capturing classes/meetings)

## Authority / Authorship
- UCSD Campus Privacy Office; inter-university review credited (UC Davis, UCOP, Maryland, Michigan, New Mexico, Yale).

## Complete clause inventory
1. Scope: Classes & Educational Meetings (not admin meetings or UCSD Health).
2. Capture allowed: disability accommodations (DCC/OSD authorized; output non-distributable; delete when no longer needed) and instructor capture with notice.
3. Capture prohibited where P-4 data (health, human-subjects, financial, undocumented-student data) or sensitive P-3 (attorney-client; animal protocols; conduct deliberations; "gender-affirming care and reproductive services") discussed; research capture needs Privacy Office+IRB+data-steward approval.
4. Office hours: "should not Capture Meetings where student concerns, behaviors, integrity issues... are intended to be discussed... chill open communications."
5. Only instructor/host may capture (absent accommodation); may allow student AI proxies to "attend" but "no one may require students to 'send' an AI assistant"; hosts may remove unattended assistants.
6. Records law: captured content = university records subject to California Public Records Act; one-year retention then deletion; approved storage only.
7. Notice standard (2 of 3: syllabus + pre-meeting reminder + at-start reminder); objection handling — "A student's refusal to participate in a Meeting with AI features enabled should not be the subject of an adverse action."
8. Approved tools only (privacy+security review, Appendix DS/GDPR agreements); biometric-retention warning.

## Sophistication markers
- The definitive AI-notetaker governance instrument in the corpus: consent granularity, protected-topic lists (incl. gender-affirming care), CPRA records logic, proxy-attendance rules, anti-retaliation.

## Classification
- **pioneer (privacy-guidance tier)** — narrow domain, best-in-class execution; multi-university peer review disclosed.
