# UCSD — Senate-Admin Workgroup Final Report on GenAI & Education (Aug 23, 2024)

## Authority / Authorship
- Senate-Administrative Workgroup (Senate + admin + student reps); charged by EVC; benchmarked against Cornell/UVA/Michigan 2023 reports.

## Complete recommendation inventory
Ethical considerations: Equity (paid-tool divide; assistive benefit for neuro-atypical students; "ensure that all of our graduates have the chance to develop AI literacy by graduation"); Integrity ("students must not offload their thinking and doing to machines unless... transparent"); Privacy (P3/P4 protection; hacks noted); Critical Thinking ("trained to predict the next best word... not accuracy"); IP (lectures distilled by students uploading transcripts = potential violation); **Academic Freedom: "we do not recommend a single, uniform policy for all of campus. Faculty purview over the curriculum should be a prioritized value."**
Recommendations: (1) Teach AI literacy (curricular integration); (2) Supportive infrastructure — permanent Senate-Admin AI in Education Committee, AI Teaching Leaders faculty corps, "Investigate the Provision of Tools," campus AI-in-Education website; (3) Protect assessment integrity; (4) Time/training/support for instructors (short + long term); (5) Continuous monitoring — plus an explicit research recommendation: "we discovered a scarcity of rigorous studies testing the effectiveness of specific uses of GenAI... support research in this area."

## Sophistication markers
- Empirical-gap honesty; faculty-purview principle as explicit anti-mandate; equity framed in both directions (access divide AND assistive benefit).

## Classification
- **pioneer (deliberation layer)** — shared-governance report of the same genus as Pitt's; both produce architecture, not rules — by design.
