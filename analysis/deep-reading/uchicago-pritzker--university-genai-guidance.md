# UChicago — University Generative AI Guidance

## Authority / Authorship
- University CIO (Kevin Boyd) & CISO (Matt Morton) as contacts; FAQ-driven guidance with approved-tools list updated "bi-monthly... by the University's Chief Technology Officer (CTO) and Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)."

## Complete clause inventory
1. "The use of confidential data with publicly available generative AI tools is prohibited without prior security and privacy review" — PII, FERPA, HIPAA, unpublished research.
2. Responsibility for accuracy/ownership of AI content ("may create citations to content that does not exist").
3. Academic integrity routed to Chicago Center for Teaching + Student Manual.
4. Procurement: security review before acquiring ANY GenAI tool "even if they are free" (Generative AI Tool Review form).
5. Review criteria: research/compliance obligations; system impact; requester context; safeguards (deletion, masking, enterprise licensing); business case; "Use of University data by vendors to train or improve their models is not permitted."
6. Failure paths: PhoenixAI alternative; approved list; Risk Acceptance Letter (RAL) escalation to CISO/CTO/CPO for out-of-tolerance uses.
7. IRB coordination for AI in human-subjects research.

## Sophistication markers
- Formal risk-acceptance instrument (RAL); bi-monthly approved-use adjudication; vendor-training-data ban; IRB workflow.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured (university IT layer)** — procurement-and-risk governance of unusual precision; academic substance delegated.
