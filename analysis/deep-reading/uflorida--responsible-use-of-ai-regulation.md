# uflorida — responsible-use-of-ai-regulation

# UF — Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence (formal regulation)

## Authority / Authorship
- University of Florida policy (policy.ufl.edu); History: New 3-24-2026; owner: Office of the Provost. Explicit scope carve-out: "does not apply to UF Health" clinical enterprises.

## Complete clause inventory
1. Definitions (AI Fluency; AI System — ISO/NIST-style machine-based inference definition; Information System; UF Activities).
2. Universal requirements: all existing regs apply equally — "conduct that is prohibited when undertaken directly by an individual is likewise prohibited when carried out by or through an AI System."
3. IRM (Integrated Risk Management) approval required "prior to installation or use" of any AI system with UF data; data-classification limits (Policy 12-011).
4. Breach/misuse reporting to CSIRT / UF Privacy.
5. Disclosure/attribution for AI-generated content in any submitted work, per applicable requirements.
6. Sensitive/restricted data only into IRM-approved systems; anonymization first.
7. Ethics: UF Ethics Policy + Florida Code of Ethics; "Fabrication, falsification or plagiarism generated or produced by AI Systems will be attributed to the user."
8. Employees: approved systems only unless written exception by Provost/CIO/VP Research; regular output checking for restricted-data or rights-affecting uses.
9. Students: "must strictly comply with the instructions of their course instructor, faculty advisor or other academic supervisor regarding the use of AI Systems" (Honor Code & Conduct Code).
10. Violations: "up to and including termination or expulsion."

## Sophistication markers
- "Same conduct, same rule" equivalence principle; state-ethics statute integration; NIST AI RMF reference; clean UF-Health carve-out acknowledging the clinical layer is separate.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured (formal university regulation)** — enacted, sanction-backed, technically neutral; the medical college's visible AI energy (separate QPSi/curriculum capture) is investment, not learner policy.
