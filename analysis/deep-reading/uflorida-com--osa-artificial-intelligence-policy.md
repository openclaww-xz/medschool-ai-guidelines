# Deep Read — UF COM OSA Artificial Intelligence Policy

## Authority / Authorship
- Office of Student Affairs policy page within COM Policies and Procedures; no date, version, or approving body stated. References the UF Honor Code as the enforcement vehicle.

## Philosophy (verbatim framing quotes)
- "The University of Florida and the College of Medicine are committed to an ethical and equitable approach to AI in teaching, research, and business practices. AI has a crucial role in the education of future physicians, medical research, and treatment of patients." (aspirational, pro-AI framing — UF's institutional AI-first reputation leaking through)
- "The UF COM requires the use of AI in a responsible, ethical, and professional manner."

## Complete Verbatim Clause Inventory (the entire policy)
1. Definition: "Artificial intelligence (AI) involves the use of computers to accomplish tasks usually require human intelligence. This broad definition refers to AI algorithms that classify, analyze, and draw predictions from data."
2. Commitment statement (above).
3. **The sole operative rule**: "Students should refer to course syllabi or the course director prior to usage of AI."
4. Enforcement: "Improper use or acknowledgement of AI for course assignments will be construed as receiving unauthorized assistance from another entity or individual in violation of the UF Honor Code."

## Sophistication Markers
- Essentially none: no GenAI/LLM specificity, no PHI clause, no disclosure mechanics, no tool approvals, no clinical dimension, no detection stance. The Honor Code-equivalence construction (AI as "another entity or individual") is the only doctrinal move.

## Distinctives
- Shortest AI policy in the corpus (≈120 words of substance).
- Total delegation to syllabi/course directors; the institution supplies only the Honor Code hook.
- The "crucial role" framing without any enabling structure is an aspirational-unregulated hybrid.

## Provisional Classification: **Conservative-naive**
Justification: minimal, generic, undated, no definitions beyond a boilerplate AI sentence, full deferral downward. Despite UF's campus-wide AI ambition (HiPerGator-era positioning), the COM OSA page is the thinnest possible compliance gesture.
