# U Iowa — AI Guidance for Students

## Authority / Authorship
- University of Iowa (ai.uiowa.edu); anchored in Academic Misconduct Policy + Code of Student Life.

## Clause inventory
1. **Governance disclosure**: "There is no single university-wide policy that determines when AI may be used for coursework. Expectations vary by instructor, assignment, department, and college."
2. "If you're unsure whether AI is permitted, ask before submitting your work."
3. "The University of Iowa's Academic Misconduct Policy prohibits the unauthorized use of artificial intelligence tools during academic exercises."
4. Student responsibilities list: understand expectations; follow course policies; complete own work unless authorized; cite/acknowledge; review accuracy; protect sensitive info.
5. "AI is a rapidly developing technology. You should regularly check on university, collegiate, and instructor guidance."

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — short, honest ask-first layer over an integrity policy that criminalizes unauthorized use; typical of the decentralized Big Ten pattern.
