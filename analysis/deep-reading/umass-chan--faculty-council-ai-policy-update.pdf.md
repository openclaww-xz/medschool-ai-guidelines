# UMass Chan — AI Policy Update to Faculty Council (May 7, 2026)

*Reclassified from hub: this is a substantive draft-policy briefing.*

## Authority / Authorship
- Bill Barnett, Chief Research Computing Officer, presenting a DRAFT AI policy to Faculty Council (May 7, 2026).
- Path: AI Subcommittee to the IT Committee → Scientific Council + academic-mission faculty review → final approval by Executive Council and Provost Flotte. Policy NOT yet signed.

## Philosophy (verbatim framing)
- "Even though most policy AI statements are addressed in other policies, it is worth specifying them in AI policies to ensure alignment in the context of AI."
- Intent: "Very general statements that align with broad principles and have application across the mission and administrative areas."

## Complete clause inventory (Main Stipulations)
1. "Use internal AI tools only with data for which they are approved" — so internal tools are used only with data from approved security levels.
2. "Do not use external tools for UMass Chan content" — keeps content/IP private, "not exposed to corporate data mining."
3. "Ensure Software with AI components is reviewed" — data protection as AI seeps into ordinary software.
4. "Review Content you use AI tools to generate" — accountability for AI-generated content.
5. "Insure you are using AI tools Ethically and Responsibly."
- Consultation: email comments incorporated into next draft; reviewed by AI Subcommittee to IT Committee.
