# UMN Medical School — Statement of Intellectual Responsibility (AI addendum reference)

## Authority / Authorship
- University of Minnesota Medical School; SIR revised by Peer Review Committee & Student Council April 2015; AI addendum referenced within Part II.

## Clause inventory
1. Community-trust philosophy ("central to any intellectual and professional endeavor is an atmosphere of mutual trust"; "one's medical education is the product of one's own intellectual effort").
2. Exams: "individual, and original, efforts only."
3. **AI pointer clause**: "Students should refer to the Addendum to the Statement of Intellectual Responsibility: use of artificial intelligence, which provides further details on the responsible use of artificial intelligence in academic work." (Addendum itself a separate document, not captured.)
4. Clinical primacy of patient welfare; privacy/confidentiality duties.
5. Peer-reporting obligation incl. good-faith uncertainty standard.
6. Signed Statement of Intent at matriculation; opt-out custom-statement path.
