# UNC — Research Generative AI Usage Guidance

## Authority / Authorship
- UNC Generative AI Committee (established by Provost and Deans, "representatives from every academic unit"); contact in Office of Research Ethics/Integrity.

## Complete clause inventory
1. Risk framing: unverifiable operations; biased/fabricated output; **privacy-equivalence principle: "Entering private or confidential information into a public AI tool is tantamount to publicly releasing that information."**
2. Norms-variability principle: "A particular use... in one research context might be perfectly appropriate, whereas an identical use... might be a serious breach."
3. Responsibility principle incl. attribution, factual accuracy, authentic sources, disclosure; supervisor duty to initiate team discussions.
4. Disclosure principle: "err on the side of explicitly disclosing any material use"; ACM's tiered disclosure policy quoted at length (prominence by proportion; appendix for whole sections; footnote for phrases; editing-assistance exemption).
5. Authorship FAQ: AI cannot meet accountability criteria (COPE/WAME/JAMA/Science positions); iThenticate screening permitted with caveats; citation-format pointers.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured (research layer)** — the ACM-proportionality adoption (like Rochester's) shows a shared source pool among research offices; the privacy-equivalence dictum is the quotable core.
