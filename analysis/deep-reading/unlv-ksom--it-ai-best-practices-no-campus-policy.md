# UNLV IT — AI Best Practices (states no campus-wide AI policy)

## Authority / Authorship
- UNLV Information Technology; explicitly situates itself under NSHE (state system) policy.

## Complete clause inventory
1. **Governance disclosure**: "There is no specific AI policy currently on campus... existing university policies already encompass the use of AI technologies"; "no UNLV-wide policies have been outlined relating to AI assessment, grading, content creation, or general use across campus."
2. Policy stack enumerated: institutional (IT standards, data privacy, research integrity, IRB, recording policy, academic misconduct); state (NRS 603A privacy, NRS 241 open meeting, NRS 239 public records); federal (FERPA, HIPAA, EO 13960, NIH, NSF).
3. "Generative AI should be treated analogously to assistance from another person"; entering assignment/exam answers "not advisable."
4. PII/credentials/non-public records never as prompts; "One should acknowledge the use of generative AI (other than incidental use) and default to disclosing such assistance when in doubt."
5. Privacy practices: anonymization; permission limitation; output monitoring (malice/bias); enterprise-vs-third-party diligence checklist (5 questions).

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — the honest "no policy here" declaration plus a serviceable ask-first stopgap; KSOM learners rely on this IT layer.
