# UNM — Appropriate Use and Guidelines (AI Resources)

## Authority / Authorship
- UNM AI Resources (airesources.unm.edu); data-steward governance model.

## Clause inventory
1. "AI tools should only be used with identifiable UNM Data classified as 'P Class' (Public) Data unless approved by the appropriate UNM Data Steward."
2. FERPA data: contact department IT officer to "initiate a risk assessment" before Data Steward approval.
3. HIPAA data: route to HIPAA Privacy Office.
4. Output verification ("inaccurate, incomplete, or biased").
5. Students comply with Pathfinder (Student Code), Faculty Handbook D100, and syllabus guidance.
6. Technology-neutral existing-policy enumeration (AUP, PII safeguarding, InfoSec, data governance, data stewards).

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — clean data-steward gating; matches the pattern of its libguide sibling and faculty-senate policy (separate notes).
