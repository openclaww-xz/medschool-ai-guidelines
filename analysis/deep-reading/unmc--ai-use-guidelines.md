# UNMC — AI Use Guidelines (Policy 8020-linked wiki)

## Authority / Authorship
- University of Nebraska Medical Center; linked to Artificial Intelligence (AI) Protections Policy No. 8020 (eff. 3/7/2025, rev. 8/19/2026); enforced via Code of Conduct, Student Code of Conduct, Research Integrity Policy; policy owner Emily Glenn (Chief Compliance Officer).

## Complete clause inventory
1. Data rule: "only public or Low Risk Data may be used with AI tools—unless a legal enterprise agreement and confidentiality agreement have been established... or the tool is listed on the UNMC Approved Technology List."
2. "Any information entered into external generative AI tools is considered public."
3. Accountability: verify errors/biases; copyright caution; research-integrity attribution for AI-generated plagiarism/fabrication.
4. Currency duty: keep abreast of agency guidance.
5. Vendor risk assessment (IM #63) for any AI in solutions.
6. NIH/NSF peer-review prohibitions quoted ("have no guarantee of where data are being sent, saved, viewed or used in the future").
7. Students: follow instructor expectations per course; "If you are permitted... you may be required to disclose your use and cite the tools... Cite AI-generated content word-for-word."
8. Faculty: "flexible framework in which faculty... can choose to prohibit, to allow with attribution, or to encourage use"; may use AI for course materials; disclosure "not required, but it is recommended to model good practice."
9. Research: funding-agency/journal compliance; "disclose and describe how you used it and identify the sections."
10. Patient data: "may not be used within AI tools" (subject to enterprise-agreement exception).

## Classification
- **progressive-structured** — compliance-office-owned guidelines with real enactment linkage (Policy 8020), tri-choice faculty framework, and modeling-good-practice norm.
