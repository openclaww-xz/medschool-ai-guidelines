# U of T Temerty Faculty of Medicine — MD Program Policies & Guidelines (index)

- Publisher: University of Toronto Temerty Faculty of Medicine, MD Program. Index page maintained by the "MD Program Accreditation Team" (named contact for Academic Regulations questions) — i.e., the accreditation apparatus owns the policy registry.
- Nature: a **navigation index** to three policy tiers (MD Program / Medical Education / Temerty Faculty), not itself a policy. Notably, no AI-specific policy appears anywhere in the index.

## (b) Policy philosophy
- Posture: traditional policy architecture. Framing verbatim: "The following list of resources outlines the **framework of policies and guidelines** relevant to MD Program students, faculty, and staff."
- Academic Regulations are organized into five categories — the taxonomy reveals the program's regulatory imagination: **Academic Integrity; Academic Standards and Promotion; Student Assessment; Student Conduct and Professionalism; Wellness and Learning Environment.**

## (d) Sophistication markers
- Negative-space marker: as of capture (2026-09-17) the MD Program's official policy index contains **no AI policy at all** — AI use at Temerty MD is governed upstream (University-level "Toward an AI-ready University" guidelines, captured separately) rather than by an MD-specific rule. Whether absence is deliberate deference or lag is undeterminable from the page.
- The five-category taxonomy shows where AI rules would have to dock (Academic Integrity; Conduct and Professionalism) if created.
