# USD Sanford — Pillar 1 Student Handbook 2025-26 (AI in Medical Education Policy)

## Authority / Authorship
- USD Sanford School of Medicine; handbook approved by Pillar I Course Director Subcommittee 12.18.2024; violations route to SSOM Student Progress and Conduct Committee.

## Complete AI-policy clause inventory
1. "Absent a clear statement from a course instructor, use of or consultation with generative AI shall be treated analogously to assistance from another person."
2. "Using generative AI tools to substantially complete an assignment or exam is not permitted."
3. "Students are expected to provide credit to any outside resource used, including AI tools. Not doing so is considered academic dishonesty" — credit may include "examples of idea formation, text, or illustrations along with a description of their methods."
4. "A student should default to disclosing such assistance when in doubt."
5. Worked citation format: "Tool Name. (Year, Month Date of Query). 'Text of Query.' Generated using Tool Name. Link to Tool Name."
6. "Students are also responsible for misinformation, disinformation, and bias in the use and/or submission of AI."

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — person-analogy default + disclosure-when-in-doubt + a concrete citation format; a compact, well-drafted handbook clause set.
