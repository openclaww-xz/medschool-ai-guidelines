# UT Austin — Acceptable Use of Generative AI Tools (ISO)

## Authority / Authorship
- Developed by Office of Legal Affairs, University Compliance Services, Information Security Office, Business Contracts Office; full revision history July 2023 → July 2026.

## Complete clause inventory
1. Public/published data usable freely.
2. Controlled/confidential data only with university-managed, contracted tools where data is not used for training, isolated instances, **"and web search functionality must be disabled."**
3. Decision Matrix for approved tools; AUP compliance.
4. Prohibited: unauthorized tools "including free or non-UT-managed versions of AI tools like ChatGPT and Copilot, **and AI Detection software**."
5. **Texas consequential-decision statute**: "Texas state law prohibits state agencies... from deploying AI intended to autonomously make or be a controlling factor in making consequential decisions without meaningful human review" — enumerated list incl. grading, admissions, discipline, "production or evaluation of: academic work product, curriculum design, instructional and testing materials," and (added 7/9/26) "medical or mental health diagnosis, treatment, or advice." CISO pre-review required.
6. Fraudulent/illegal-use ban.
7. Personal click-through liability warning (delegation-of-signature authority).

## Sophistication markers
- AI-detection software classified as *prohibited AI*; web-search-must-be-disabled contract term; statutory consequential-decision mapping with dated amendments; revision history as governance artifact.

## Classification
- **progressive-structured (state-regulatory IT policy)** — the sharpest state-law-driven instrument in the corpus; Texas HB-style human-review mandates embedded operationally.
