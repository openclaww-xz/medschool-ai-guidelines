# Utah — AI use guidelines from an IT leadership perspective

## Authority / Authorship
- Signed by Donna Roach (U of U Health CIO) and Steve Hess (U of U CIO); companion to the enterprise guidelines (run 29).

## Clause inventory
1. Public AI tools "fall within the public domain and lack security and data regulation compliance features" — never enter sensitive/restricted data per Rule 4-004C (PHI, FERPA, PII, donor, IP, PCI).
2. BAA requirement: "A Business Associate Agreement (BAA) must be on file with the University of Utah Health Privacy Office for AI-related and other IT products/vendors that store or process PHI."
3. Misinformation: outputs "must be verified, and the AI tool cited."
4. Bias review-and-edit duty.
5. Copyright: review for "copyrighted, without proper attribution"; "AI tools should never be used in place of healthcare recommendations and peer-reviewed research."
6. AI as "continuous learning tool... does not replace it."
7. Incident reporting (iso@utah.edu).
8. Cross-links to research/instructional/data-privacy guidance.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed (dual-CIO health-system guidance)** — BAA-on-file operationalism is the distinctive clause; the health-sciences specificity here compensates for the enterprise guideline's breadth.
