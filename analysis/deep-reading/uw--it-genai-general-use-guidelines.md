## Clause inventory
1. "Only UW-approved services and applications may be used with University data"; UWare catalog; unauthorized release = potential policy/law violation with personal liability.
2. Students: "Course instructors have the discretion to allow, prohibit, or put parameters around the use of GenAI."
3. Harm-minimization: review outputs; "GenAI may enhance, but does not replace, human judgment, particularly in decision-making affecting individuals"; avoid bias reinforcement.
4. Transparency/records: "Disclosing GenAI use is often advisable and may be mandatory"; "Maintain detailed records of GenAI use"; TrustArc personal-data inventory; records-retention law compliance.
5. Data: Level 1 public OK; Levels 2-4 restricted absent controls/contracts.
6. High-risk uses (hiring, admissions, performance evaluations) → privacy impact assessment (uwprivacy@).
7. Rich external-resource awareness (Washington State AG AI Task Force, Seattle/Tacoma city policies, EDUCAUSE, Internet2).
