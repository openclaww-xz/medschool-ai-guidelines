# UW-Madison — GenAI use & policies (IT, v3 Aug 2024)

## Authority / Authorship
- UW-Madison Information Technology; maps AI use onto UW-523/504, UWSA SYS1031, Regent Policy 25-3.

## Complete prohibited-use inventory (each mapped to policies)
1. No sensitive/restricted/protected data into any GenAI tool absent review — enumerated: FERPA (Wiscard photos, directory data, "Work produced by students to satisfy course requirements," grades, disability info), HIPAA health info, employee-performance info, non-public IP, "Material under confidential review, including research papers and funding proposals," export-controlled info.
2. No uploading files you're not authorized to access (UW-870).
3. No malware/phishing facilitation (Regent 25-3).
4. No aerial photos/scaled drawings of campus (UW-6060 — physical-security clause unique in corpus).
5. "You may not use AI-generated code within institutional IT systems... without having it reviewed by a human" (UW-503).
6. No sexual-harassment/stalking content (UW-146); no law-breaking content; no harassment/defamation.
7. No copyright infringement — "Entering copyrighted material into a generative AI tool or service may effectively result in the creation of a digital copy... Feeding copyrighted material into a generative AI tool could 'train' the AI to output works that violate" rights; patent-premature-disclosure risk.
8. Incident-reporting duties (UW-509, SYS1033, UW-131 HIPAA).
9. Trustworthy-AI pointer (NIST characteristics).

## Classification
- **progressive-structured (policy-mapping table)** — the densest policy-by-reference instrument in the guidance cohort; effectively a crosswalk converting all existing IT/conduct policy into AI terms.
