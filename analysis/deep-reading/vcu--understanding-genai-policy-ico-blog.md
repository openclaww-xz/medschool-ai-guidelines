# VCU ICO blog — Understanding VCU's GenAI Policy (Mar 13, 2026)

## Authority / Authorship
- VCU Integrity and Compliance Office (Catherine Kiesel); explains the Nov 12, 2025 Acceptable Use of Generative AI Technology policy.

## Clause inventory
1. Tone: "AI is like the wild west – there's a lot of unknown still."
2. Policy goals: protect confidential data; support academic/research integrity; ensure compliance; foster digital literacy.
3. Key points: data-privacy gating; academic integrity (faculty syllabi clarity; student accountability; verification and attribution; "Using AI tools in ways that are dishonest, or misrepresent student effort, is considered academic misconduct"); accuracy/copyright caution; **FOIA reminder**: "Conversations with AI that involve VCU-related business may be available for public record requests under the Freedom of Information Act... even chats generated on personal accounts or devices."
4. Training/approved tools via CIO's office.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed (compliance-communication layer)** — the FOIA/public-records clause is the distinctive (Virginia public-records law surfaced for AI chats).
