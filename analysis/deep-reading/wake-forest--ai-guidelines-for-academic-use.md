# Wake Forest — AI Guidelines for Academic Use (AIWG final report guidance)

## Authority / Authorship
- Provost's AI Working Group for Academics (Nov 2023-Feb 2025; 17 named members incl. CIO, librarians, deans, Honor & Ethics Council judicial liaison); final report to Provost/Deans March 2025.

## Complete clause inventory (recommendations layer)
1. University-wide expectations: clear class guidance in every class; honor codes may "set new expectations for GAI-related academic misconduct."
2. Syllabus statement expectations (permitted/not; required uses; disclosure norms; consequences).
3. **Equity/access obligations**: "when GAI is taught or used in assignments, instructors have a responsibility to ensure that students will have equitable access to tools and resources, including options for disability accommodations"; cost transparency "following existing university processes for required course materials"; "use university-licensed or university-recommended free tools whenever possible"; alternatives for students objecting on privacy grounds.
4. Accessibility program: capacity-building (IS Technology Accessibility, CLASS, ITG); student-facing barrier guides; multi-modal accessibility uses.
5. Professional development; **GAI Discussion Framework** — structured departmental conversation topics (integrity & learning; multi-section consensus; assessment redesign: "how GAI may affect typical disciplinary assessments... whether that projected impact requires... alternative measures for making learning visible"; equity; GAI-as-professional-skill: "departments include instruction in GAI use and best practices somewhere in their curriculum"; faculty work incl. letters-of-recommendation uses; tenure/promotion implications).
6. Administrative-use guidelines cross-reference.

## Sophistication markers
- The Discussion Framework is a governance instrument disguised as a conversation guide — program-level calibration with annual-report follow-through; equity obligations are the strongest in the guidance cohort.

## Classification
- **pioneer (deliberation-to-guidance layer)** — the working-group report operationalized; Wake Forest's model is faculty-conversation-based governance rather than rules.
