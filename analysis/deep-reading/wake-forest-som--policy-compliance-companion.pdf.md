## Authority / Authorship

## Complete AI clause inventory (all 3 clusters)
**DAN Honor Code (MD/graduate programs):**
1. Plagiarism definition includes "the use of artificial intelligence, and claiming as your own work."
2. "Adhere to the DAN artificial intelligence (AI) use and applicable privacy guidelines."
3. **Structured AI-use section**: "Students may use AI tools for educational support and academic enrichment when their use is: (1) Transparent: Disclosed when used in coursework. (2) Ethical: Does not replace original student thinking or authorship. (3) Compliant: Adheres to privacy and regulatory standards, including FERPA and HIPAA."
4. "Students must ensure all submitted academic and clinical work reflects personal understanding and learning."
5. Acceptable-use examples: grammar/spelling; "Summarizing or synthesizing scholarly sources"; "Practicing clinical reasoning in AI-enabled simulations (as approved by faculty)"; outlining/study organization.
6. Prohibited: submitting AI work as original; "Compromises clinical reasoning or patient safety"; disclosing student records/PHI/faculty-peer-staff info/clinical documentation/site details/simulation content; "Circumvents assessment requirements."
7. Third-party-tool responsibility clause: students must understand privacy practices, recognize "content entered may be stored, analyzed, or used to train algorithms," and evaluate "reliability, bias, and appropriateness."
**PA Program honor code:**
8. Plagiarism includes "the use of artificial intelligence (e.g.: ChatGPT or CoPilot) and claiming as your own work."
**Evaluation protocol:**
9. "AI-enabled devices" banned in testing areas alongside smart watches/phones.
