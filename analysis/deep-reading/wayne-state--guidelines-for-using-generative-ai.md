# Wayne State C&IT — Guidelines for using generative AI

## Authority / Authorship
- Wayne State Computing & Information Technology (tech.wayne.edu policies).

## Clause inventory
1. Definitions (AI/GenAI/LLM, Gartner-style).
2. Non-discouragement framing: "do not intend to discourage the use of technology that contains AI functionality, but to allow for the safe exploration."
3. Data security: avoid non-public data; examples enumerated — enterprise reporting outputs (Cognos/Power BI/Banner/ODS/STARS), FERPA student data **"including grading student assignments or identifying work of a student that may be been generated by AI"**, PII/PHI/financial/HR/research data with cell size <10, copyright/trademark, recordings/transcripts.
6. AI meeting companions: C&IT approval required; third-party bots not approved; waiting-room bot-deny tactic; editing-before-publishing duty.
7. Research: NIH/NSF policy compliance; no research documentation into unapproved tools; PI responsibility.
8. Approved-tools list pointer; vendor-validation caveat (opt-out varies, "should be validated with the vendor").

## Sophistication markers
- Small-cell-size (<10) research-data rule (statistical disclosure control); bot-deny tactics; FERPA-grading-via-AI flagged.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed (IT guidelines)** — the SOM's 2025 handbook AI policy (announcement captured separately) sits above this; C&IT layer is data-careful and tactically specific.
