# WCM Library — Ethics, Policy & Responsible Use of AI (LibGuide)

## Authority / Authorship
- Weill Cornell Medicine Medical College Library; WCM/NYP context.

## Complete clause inventory
1. "At Weill Cornell Medicine and NewYork-Presbyterian, our community is trusted with sensitive information."
2. Core principles: Transparency ("Disclose when AI is used in research, teaching, or publication"); Accountability; Fairness & Bias; Privacy ("Never input PHI/PII or sensitive data"); "Integrity in Education — Students should use AI as a study aid, not as a replacement for original work."
3. Dos: Copilot for summarizing/brainstorming; cite/acknowledge substantive use; verify vs peer-reviewed sources; "Ask your librarian."
4. Don'ts: enter PHI/PII/student records/confidential data; "Use AI tools to make clinical decisions or replace evidence-based practice"; treat outputs as fact (hallucination); assume uniform publisher policies; list AI as an author.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed (library layer)** — tight dos/don'ts format; the anti-clinical-decision don't is the med-relevant core; WCM's curricular MedEdAI layer is separate.
