# WCM — MedEdAI @ WCM (AI competencies in medical education)

## Authority / Authorship
- Weill Cornell Medicine (teach.weill.cornell.edu); consensus-based competencies "through a collaborative, interdisciplinary process that engaged faculty, informatics experts, institutional leaders, and learners."

## Clause inventory
1. Philosophy: "artificial intelligence is incorporated into medical education as a thoughtful, human-centered integration... prepares students to critically engage with AI, use it responsibly in practice, and contribute to its ongoing development."
2. Competency domains: Foundational Knowledge ("Describe basic concepts in AI, ML, and data science relevant to clinical medicine"); Clinical Applications ("Carry out AI-enhanced clinical encounters... maintaining clinical judgment and accountability").
3. Curricular evidence: "Health Informatics and Decision Support—Artificial Intelligence in Health Care" senior refresher (LLM mechanics; documentation/diagnostic support/patient communication applications; regulatory landscape); **MedSimAI** — "From their first weeks in medical school, students engage with MedSimAI, an AI-powered simulation platform... deliberate practice, self-regulated learning, and automated assessment through interactive patient encounters."

## Classification
- **pioneer (curriculum-competency layer)** — WCM's AI posture is a competency framework + first-weeks AI simulation, not a conduct policy; the strongest pure-curriculum approach in the guidance cohort.
