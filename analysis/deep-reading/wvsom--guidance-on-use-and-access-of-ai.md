# WVSOM — Guidance on Use and Access of AI (IT security)

## Authority / Authorship
- WVSOM IT Security; aligns to Institutional Policy GA-31 (acceptable/unacceptable data & technology use); OGC coordination for tool-legality review.

## Complete clause inventory
1. Universal policy subordination (academic integrity, professionalism).
2. Accuracy/copyright review duty.
3. No sensitive/private/restricted/confidential data (PII/PHI); data-risk categorization page; "public" consumption only.
4. IP safeguards; IT consultation for new AI tools; "Director of Infrastructure and IT Security will coordinate with the Office of the General Counsel for a review of the AI tool, whether free or purchased."
5. Transparency test: "Always ask yourself if a reasonable person would expect to know that you used generative AI."
6. **Unauthorized-practice-of-law clause**: AI "cannot be used to give legal advice, prepare legal documents... This constitutes the unauthorized practice of law and can result in disciplinary action, and civil or criminal penalties." (Unique in corpus.)
7. Curriculum rules: acceptable = notes efficiency, study plans, idea generation, problem-solving platforms; unacceptable = "bypass learning, provide answers without student engagement or logical reasoning, complete graded assignments without student input, assistance with any summative assessment without course director approval."
8. Course-director designation required in syllabus; absent designation = no AI without prior approval.
9. Domain permitted/prohibited example tables (communications, analytics, document management, customer service...).

## Classification
- **progressive-structured** — small school, big clause set: UPL clause, reasonable-person transparency test, syllabus-designation mechanic; the strongest AI web hub in the osteopathic cohort.
