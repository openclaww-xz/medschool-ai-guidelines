# Yale Provost — Guidelines for the Use of Generative AI Tools (Sept 20, 2023)

## Authority / Authorship
- Office of the Provost, message to the Yale community; the foundational 2023 university guidelines (health-sciences follow-ons captured separately).

## Clause inventory (all six, verbatim structure)
1. "Protect Yale's confidential information and your own" — nothing ≥ moderate/high-risk per data classification policy.
2. "Assume all information shared will be made public... be mindful that the information you input into an AI tool may be retained."
3. Academic integrity: know your school's policies; "Faculty members are expected to provide clear instructions on the permitted use of generative AI tools for academic work and requirements for attribution"; students follow instructor guidelines.
4. "Be alert for bias and inaccuracy" — "We are each responsible for the content of our work product."
5. "Protect yourself and your credentials" — never share NetID/password with AI tools; phishing awareness.
6. "Seek support" — procurement via purchasing intake; initial security review of any AI product.

## Classification
- **cautious-informed** — the canonical 2023 scaffold (confidentiality/publicity/integrity/bias/credentials/procurement); no clinical layer, which arrives later at ai.yale.edu health-sciences pages (separate notes).
