At Hartford HealthCare, we're not waiting for the future of healthcare — we're building it.
This is the place where bold ideas become real-world breakthroughs. We're rethinking what healthcare can be by focusing on what matters most: Access, Affordability, Health Equity, and Excellence — our A2E2 strategy.
By collaborating with top-tier institutions, tech pioneers, and research leaders, we turn vision into action.
Your idea can help us shape the next wave of innovation.
Join us in shaping what's next in the future of healthcare.
The Center for AI Innovation was launched in February 2024.
The space is focused on unlocking the transformative power of artificial intelligence (AI) for our patients in a safe and trustworthy way.
The Center is the first of its kind in New England, and one of only a few in the United States. It has developed 6 core pillars over the last 9 years.
The Center is a culmination of ongoing collaborations with prestigious institutions, such as the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) and the University of Oxford in the United Kingdom.
