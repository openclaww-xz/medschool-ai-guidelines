Our Mission

The mission of the Howard University AI in Healthcare Center is to facilitate the development and application of artificial intelligence (AI) algorithms to the delivery of healthcare, especially focusing on minority populations. The center is made up of a group of AI experts, clinicians, healthcare experts, researchers, and administrators who conduct healthcare related research and operations projects (focusing on people from minority populations) in the intersection of AI, healthcare, and public health
