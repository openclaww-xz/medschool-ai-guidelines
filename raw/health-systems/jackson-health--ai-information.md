Jackson Health System is a nonprofit academic medical system serving Miami-Dade County, Florida, and one of the largest public health systems in the United States. Founded in 1918 as Miami City Hospital, it is anchored by Jackson Memorial Hospital and governed by the Public Health Trust on behalf of the Miami-Dade Board of County Commissioners. Jackson operates seven hospitals, more than 2,600 beds, and a countywide network of urgent care, specialty, behavioral health, and long-term care facilities, providing a single high standard of care to all Miami-Dade residents regardless of their ability to pay.

What Jackson Health System Does

Jackson Health System is Miami-Dade County’s public academic medical system. Its mission is to build the health of the community by providing a single, high standard of quality care for the residents of Miami-Dade County — including patients regardless of their ability to pay.

Jackson is an integrated healthcare delivery system: it operates acute-care hospitals, a children’s hospital, a behavioral health hospital, a rehabilitation center, urgent care centers, primary and specialty care centers, two long-term care nursing facilities, and Corrections Health Services for Miami-Dade County.

As the primary teaching hospital system for the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine, Jackson combines public safety-net care with academic medicine, training roughly 1,100 residents and fellows each year.

Medical Services

Jackson’s major service lines include trauma (Ryder Trauma Center, a Level 1 trauma center), organ transplant (Miami Transplant Institute), burn treatment, cardiac care, maternity and newborn care, pediatrics, bariatric (weight-loss) surgery, rehabilitation, behavioral health, primary care through Jackson Medical Group, and emergency care at ERs in Miami, Doral, and North Miami. Community Role & Funding

Jackson is a safety-net system: it provides care to all Miami-Dade residents regardless of ability to pay, supported in part by Miami-Dade County public funding, including a voter-approved half-penny sales tax (passed 1991). The system has raised more than $350 million since it began in 1987. Jackson Health Foundation, the system’s fundraising arm, supports patient care needs at
https://jacksonhealthfoundation.org
.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Jackson Health System?

Jackson Health System is a nonprofit academic medical system and public safety-net provider serving Miami-Dade County, Florida. Founded in 1918, it operates six hospitals — anchored by Jackson Memorial Hospital — plus urgent care centers, specialty centers, behavioral health, and long-term care facilities.

Is Jackson Health System a public or private hospital system?

Jackson is a nonprofit, tax-assisted public health system governed by the Public Health Trust on behalf of Miami-Dade County. It provides care to all county residents regardless of their ability to pay.

Is Jackson Health System affiliated with the University of Miami?

Yes. Jackson has been the primary teaching hospital system for the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine since the school opened in 1952. Many programs operate jointly under the UHealth/Jackson name, but Jackson Health System and the University of Miami are separate organizations.

Where are Jackson Health System’s emergency rooms?

Jackson operates emergency rooms in Miami (Jackson Memorial, with a new ER entrance), Doral (Jackson West), North Miami (Jackson North), and South Miami-Dade (Jackson South). For life-threatening emergencies, call 911.

Does Jackson Health System offer financial assistance?

Yes. Jackson provides financial assistance programs for eligible Miami-Dade residents and publishes details at
https://jacksonhealth.org/patient-tools/financial-assistance/
.

Can I get a price estimate from Jackson before treatment?

Yes. Jackson offers patient price estimators with approximate out-of-pocket costs, including gross charges, discounted cash prices, and payer-specific negotiated rates, at
https://jacksonhealth.org/patient-tools/billing-and-payments/
.

Does Jackson Health System treat international patients?

Yes. Jackson has an international patient program; details are at
https://jacksonhealth.org/patient-tools/international-patients/
.

What is Ryder Trauma Center?

Ryder Trauma Center, located at Jackson Memorial Hospital, is a Level 1 trauma center that opened in 1992. It is the U.S. Army’s only national Army Trauma Training Center and is the designated South Florida facility to treat the President of the United States if necessary.
