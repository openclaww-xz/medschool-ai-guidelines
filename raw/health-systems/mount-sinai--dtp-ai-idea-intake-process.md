DTP AI Idea Submission Process
                                  FAQs and Guideline


A. OVERVIEW

All Project, Product and Solutions with an Artificial Intelligence component must
follow the Mount Sinai Health System’s policy and guidelines regarding Ethical AI
Usage. Any project requiring AI development, research, or system implementation
will be reviewed by the AI Review Board (AIRB).

The AI Review Board is responsible for ensuring clinical and operational AI initiatives
are safe, effective, equitable, and ethical. AI projects align with business standards,
data security protocols, and operational feasibility. This document outlines the
process for submitting a project request and the subsequent review by the AI Rew
Board.

B. SUBMITTING AN AI PROJECT REQUEST

What types of projects need AIRB approval?
Projects involving AI technologies, including machine learning models, natural
language processing, or automated systems that interact with Mount Sinai Health
System, require review by the AIRB.

How do I submit a request to the AIRB?
You can submit your project request via the ServiceNow DTP AI Idea intake form
portal. The submission form will guide you through entering the required information,
including the business case of the project, value proposition, and potential benefits.

What information is required for submission?
The submission form will ask for the following details:

   •   Contact information
   •   Project overview
   •   Business case
   •   FDA/HIPPA compliance (if applicable)
   •   Implementation strategy
   •   Security and privacy protocols
   •   Expected outcomes and ROI

C. EVALUATION PROCESS

What happens after submission?
Once a project is submitted, the AI Governance Team will conduct an initial pre-triage
to assess and review the request. This review will determine if a similar project or

solution is already in use within the organization. The team will also check for
incomplete or insufficient submissions. If necessary, the form will be returned with
feedback for revision. If the submission is complete and accepted, it will be scheduled
for review by the AI Review Board (AIRB).

How often does the AIRB meet?
The AIRB convenes twice monthly. After submission, you will receive a confirmation
email with the expected review date. Requesters will be invited to participate in the
review meeting to present their request.

Who needs to attend the AIRB review meeting?
The project requester, sponsor and/or a designated team member is required to attend
the AIRB meeting to present a brief overview of the project and answer questions from
the committee. Other relevant team members, such as subject matter experts (SMEs),
clinicians, and stakeholders, may also participate if applicable. However, vendors are
not permitted to attend any AIRB meetings.

What happens during the AIRB meeting?
During the meeting, the project sponsor will provide a 5–10 minute overview.
Afterward, AIRB members will ask questions regarding the project’s feasibility, data
handling, success criteria and potential risks. Once the requester leaves the meeting,
the committee will deliberate and vote to either approve the project for Proof of
Concept (POC), request further information, or deny it.

When will I be notified of the AIRB’s decision?
You will receive notification of the AIRB’s decision within 5-7 business days after the
scheduled meeting.

D. VALIDATION PROCESS

How is the project validated?
Validation is carried out through a Proof of Concept (POC) using predefined metrics to
analyze data and assess results. The requestor will determine the appropriate
timeframe to gather meaningful results and will track the POC against these metrics.
Once the POC is complete, the requestor will return to the AIRB to present the
outcomes and discuss next steps for deployment. When ready, the requestor will
contact the DTP team to schedule a review with the AI Review Board.

What happens during deployment?
During deployment, the requestor should be prepared to present the POC results,
ensuring that all relevant data is accurate and available for the AI Review Board’s
evaluation. The board will use this information to score the AI solution according to
the Gartner Rubric. Based on the assessment, the board will either approve or deny
the project for deployment.

Please note: If the solution involves a new application, the DTP Intake team will
manage the intake process in parallel.

F. QUALITY ASSURANCE

Ongoing validation and testing of success metrics will take place post-deployment.
Requestors will be required to present the project’s performance to the AI Review
Board 6-9 months later for a quality assurance review. During this period, AI model
outputs and success metrics will be continuously tested, monitored, and audited
through validation and assurance testing.
