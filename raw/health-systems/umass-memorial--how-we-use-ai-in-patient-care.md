How We Use AI in Patient Care

UMass Memorial Health uses artificial intelligence tools to support patient care and certain daily work. These tools are designed to assist our staff with specific tasks, while people remain fully in charge. AI does not replace a staff member’s clinical judgment. It’s used only to support, not direct, care. As the U.S. population continues to age and the supply of healthcare workers declines, AI can help address this growing care gap. AI enhances how our caregivers work, so they can focus even more on you and your needs.
Examples of How We Use AI
Create medical notes from conversations during patient appointments
: This allows staff members to focus more on listening and engaging with patients during visits rather than typing notes. The staff member carefully reviews the notes to ensure accuracy before the notes become part of the medical record.
Review diagnostic images
: This supports staff members as they evaluate test results.
Help to identify emergency department patients who may need urgent attention after their initial evaluation
: This allows staff members to focus first on the most serious cases so patients can receive care as quickly as possible.
Review patient messages and elevate those that need quick attention
: This helps providers respond faster.
Our Commitment to Responsible and Secure AI Use
We’re committed to using AI responsibly. Every digital tool we use, including AI tools, is thoroughly evaluated before it’s approved for use. When patient information is used with approved AI tools, it’s protected by the same privacy, security, and confidentiality safeguards that apply to all patient information at UMass Memorial Health. AI tools are used only for specific purposes and don’t have unrestricted access to patient information. Not all AI tools are used in every care setting or for every patient.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is artificial intelligence?

AI is technology that helps computers learn from information in a way that is like how humans learn. It can help people make better decisions by handling repetitive or complex tasks. AI doesn’t think or make decisions about patient care by itself. Staff members must review and control how it’s used.

How does UMass Memorial Health ensure AI is used responsibly?

UMass Memorial carefully reviews AI tools before, during, and after their use for clinical or business purposes. This process focuses on appropriate uses. Only approved tools are used and with human oversight.

How does AI support patient care?

AI can help support patient care by simplifying complex tasks, organizing information and highlighting important clinical information to improve efficiency.

Is artificial intelligence replacing the role of healthcare professionals?

No. Artificial intelligence isn’t replacing healthcare professionals. Human oversight is critical when using AI tools and making sure that the information is correct.
