Policies & Guidelines

The following list of resources outlines the framework of policies and guidelines relevant to MD Program students, faculty, and staff, including MD Program, Medical Education, and the Temerty Faculty of Medicine policies, guidelines, regulations, and statements.

If you have any questions regarding MD Program Academic Regulations or are having difficulty locating specific information related to policies, procedures, or guidelines please
contact the MD Program Accreditation Team
.

MD Program Academic Regulations

Policies, statements and guidelines relevant to the MD Program and that apply to all University of Toronto medical students are available within the
Academic Calendar
.

These Academic Regulations are organized into five categories:

Academic Integrity

Academic Standards and Promotion

Student Assessment

Student Conduct and Professionalism

Wellness and Learning Environment

Medical Education Policies & Guidelines

Medial Education guidelines and statements that apply to learners across Temerty Medicine, including medical students, residents and fellows can be located on the
Medical Education webpage
.

Faculty members can also find guidelines relating to teaching performance and the support process on this website.
