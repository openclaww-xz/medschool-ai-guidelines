Artificial Intelligence in Pediatric Health Care

The development and application of artificial intelligence tools in health care is growing rapidly. AAP is creating and sharing resources to help pediatricians and pediatric specialists understand and utilize these tools to decrease burden, promote health equity, and improve the overall quality of pediatric health care.

AAP AI in Pediatrics Webinar Series

Generative Artificial Intelligence: What Pediatricians and Pediatric Specialists Need to Know
Dr Srinivasan Suresh presents a comprehensive overview of generative AI in health care, with a focus on potential applications in the care of children.

Generative AI for Medical Education in Pediatrics
Dr Karim Hanna describes and demonstrates AI-enhanced approaches to medical education.

Generative AI for Clinical Scenarios in Pediatrics
Dr Yaa Kumah-Crystal describes and demonstrates potential use cases for generative AI tools to support patient engagement and understanding in clinical settings.

Generative AI for Administrative Tasks in Pediatrics
Dr Naveed Rabbani describes and demonstrates the potential value of generative AI tools for administrative tasks in pediatric health care.

Frequently Asked Questions About AI in Pediatrics - Part 1
Dr Srinivasan Suresh and Dr Gregg Lund answer pediatricians' questions about generative AI tools in pediatric care.

AI in Pediatric Health Care Research Part 2
An engaging discussion of how busy clinicians can use AI tools to understand, keep up with, and share the latest in pediatric health care research.

AI in Pediatrics: Works in Progress
Listen to learn how pediatricians and pediatric specialists are using AI tools and technology in innovative ways. You will hear from people who are building and implementing tools in exciting ways to support children and families.
