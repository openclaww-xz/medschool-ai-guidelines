Best Practices

When using generative AI tools, it's essential to comply fully with existing policies and legal requirements:

Do not use generative AI applications to generate clinical documentation, such as medical records or letters.

Avoid utilizing generative AI applications with any form of protected health information or other legally protected data.

Refrain from employing generative AI applications for clinical or human subjects research, even if the data has been anonymized.

Keep confidential business information away from generative AI applications.

Copyright Considerations

Intellectual property law across the globe is reckoning with the implications of generative AI for creators of copyrighted works and the artists, programmers, and scholars who would use those works for generative AI outputs. Many questions are implicated, including the copyrightability, authorship, and ownership of generative AI outputs; the merits of a fair use defense when using copyrighted works to train AI tools; and what regulations, if any, should be imposed in response to this dramatic shift in technology and society. At this time, there is not a clear answer to many of these questions.
