Data Security

Know Your Data, Choose the Right Tool

Before picking a tool for institutional work, you must know two things: what kind of data you're working
with, and which tools are actually cleared for it. Here's a quick primer.

Know Your Data

Not all data carries the same risk. Dartmouth security controls (DISC) sort data into four levels:
Public (0), Low (1), Medium (2), and High (3). If you're working with patient information, grades,
disciplinary records, etc., your data is High (DISC 3).

A Practical Guide to DISC Classification →

Choose the Right Tool

Not every AI tool is cleared for every security level. The AI Data Sensitivity Guide linked below will
provide you the latest guidance on our enterprise tools. However, users whose data is subject to or
adjacent to HIPAA, FERPA, or FCI/CUI should seek an information security review before beginning
their work.

AI Data Sensitivity Guide →

A Few Rules of Thumb

Good gut check: if you would hesitate to include this data in an email to people outside Dartmouth, you should be
very
cautious about disclosing it to AI tools.

Turn off "web search" features when working with sensitive or regulated data. AI-initiated searches could expose content in the search prompts they create/use.

ALWAYS confirm you are logged in with your Dartmouth NetID to any AI tool being used for College work. Personal accounts (even with known vendors like ChatGPT or Gemini) are not protected by the same controls Dartmouth has instituted with our vendors.

IRB Considerations:

interaction, consult with
Dartmouth CPHS
early.

Grants & Funding:
NIH, NSF, and other funders have issued guidance on AI use in funded research.
Check your program announcement and your funder's current AI policy before using AI tools in grant-related work.

Manuscripts & Publications:
Most major journals require disclosure of AI tool use
in manuscript preparation. Check your target journal's author guidelines. When in doubt, disclose.

Formal Dartmouth and Geisel guidance on AI in research is in development. Check back here for updates.
