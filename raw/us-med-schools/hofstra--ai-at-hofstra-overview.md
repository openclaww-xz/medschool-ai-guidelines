GIVING IT A TEST RUN

ChatGPT Edu Pilot Program

In the 2024 academic year, Hofstra launched a pilot program for select faculty and staff to request a ChatGPT Edu license, supporting uses from instruction to research to operational tasks.

Some use cases:

Disability Studies

ChatGPT can create sufficiently detailed personas or character sketches (including appearance, occupation, and backstory) of individuals, allowing students to better understand the challenges and barriers that affect people with disabilities.

Counseling and Mental Health Professions

Developed practice simulations that offered graduate counseling students the opportunity to engage in simulated counseling sessions using ChatGPT as a mock client.

Creative Arts Therapy Counseling

AI helped develop new, up-to-date terminology for intake paperwork, biopsychosocial, and information data sheets.

Communication

Used ChatGPT Edu for implementation of a quick BINGO game for class review.

Axinn Library

Explored the use of AI in grant proposal writing, to mixed results.

Population Health

Students compared symptom interpretation between ChatGPT Edu, Google Search, and a clinician.

Journalism

To inject life into a topic perceived as dull, a semester-long “game show” was created, with problem-solving competitions judged by ChatGPT Edu, with real-time feedback provided during class.

Why Use Your Hofstra ChatGPT Edu Account?

Hofstra ChatGPT Edu is a secure, education-focused version of OpenAI’s ChatGPT that the University is offering to all students, faculty, and administrators. Unlike the public version, prompts and chats remain private and are not shared outside Hofstra. OpenAI also does not use data entered into Hofstra ChatGPT Edu to train its models. By providing this tool campuswide, the University is helping the community to build AI literacy and prepare for a workforce where AI skills are increasingly essential.

Why is Hofstra providing access to ChatGPT Edu?

We want every member of the Hofstra community to be ready for the future, and incorporating AI into teaching and learning will open the door to more classes and across disciplines. Artificial intelligence is rapidly transforming the professional landscape in the U.S. and internationally. Employers across all business sectors have a growing expectation that new employees be proficient in artificial intelligence tools such as ChatGPT and understand how to use them creatively, effectively, and ethically.

What can I do with Hofstra’s ChatGPT Edu accounts?

ChatGPT Edu can be an effective tool to support brainstorming, drafting, and exploring ideas. It should be used to support, not replace, your own judgment, analysis, and decision-making. All outputs from Hofstra ChatGPT Edu should be reviewed by the user, and revised, if appropriate, before use or distribution.

Students might use ChatGPT Edu to:

Brainstorm ideas for essays, presentations, or projects

Outline study guides or summarize course readings (with verification of accuracy)

Draft practice questions for exam preparation

Rephrase complex material in simpler language to aid understanding

Explore different perspectives on a topic before forming their own argument

Faculty might use ChatGPT Edu to:

Draft discussion prompts, essay questions, or rubrics (with personal review before use)

Design lesson plan ideas and classroom activities

Summarize scholarly articles to aid teaching preparation

Generate accessibility aids, such as draft alternative text for images

Administrators and eligible staff might use ChatGPT Edu to:

Draft communications such as emails, memos, or announcements (with review before distribution)

Brainstorm strategies for events, outreach, or projects

Summarize meeting notes or lengthy reports into action items

Create templates for routine documents, such as FAQ handouts or guides

Explore planning scenarios to support decision-making (while confirming outcomes with human oversight)

How do I know if I need advanced features?

All eligible Hofstra ChatGPT Edu users have unlimited access to OpenAI’s GPT-5 model. Faculty and staff may request access to advanced models and features, including GPT-5 Pro, Deep Research, and Image Generation. To submit a request, send an email to
help@hofstra.edu
with the specific feature requested and a description of your use case. Availability of advanced features may change and is subject to licensing limitations.

Is there any cost to using ChatGPT Edu at Hofstra?

Your Hofstra ChatGPT Edu account provides unlimited access to GPT-5, the latest model, at no additional cost to you. Advanced features, such as Deep Research, image generation, and others, may incur a cost in the form of usage credits.

What happens after a student graduates?

ChatGPT Edu accounts will be deleted upon graduation or withdrawal from the University.

Is the University monitoring my chats?

As with all Hofstra-provided technology services, individual accounts are not routinely monitored. However, the University reserves the right to review the content of accounts when necessary to maintain system security, investigate suspected policy violations, or comply with the law.
