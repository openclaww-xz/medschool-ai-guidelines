Frequently Asked Questions

What is generative AI?

Generative AI (GenAI) is a category of AI systems/algorithms that can generate new content, such as text, images, audio, or code, based on patterns learned from existing data. This includes models like GPT (text generation) and GANs (image generation).

What are the primary applications of generative AI?

Applications of generative AI range from content creation (e.g., writing, art, music), gaming and virtual environments, drug discovery, and molecular design, to personalized education and conversational agents.

Who can benefit from generative AI at Johns Hopkins?

Faculty, staff, researchers, and students can all benefit from generative AI through educational resources, collaborative projects, and research opportunities.
