Artificial intelligence is reshaping health care and medical training at an unprecedented pace, and the University of Miami Miller School of Medicine is equipping learners and educators with the tools to harness it responsibly. The AI in Medical Education Resources page centralizes innovative guides, instructional frameworks, toolkits, and best practices designed to support ethical and effective integration of AI into medical teaching, learning, assessment, and scholarly work. Whether you are exploring personalized learning pathways, discovering AI-enhanced educational tools, or seeking frameworks for safe and equitable use, these curated resources empower the Miller School community to lead in AI-driven medical education and prepare future physicians for a data-rich, technology-enabled world.
