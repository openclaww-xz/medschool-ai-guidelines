# Artificial Intelligence: Policies & Guidelines
**Overview**
Morehouse College is building an AI-ready culture that elevates teaching, learning, and research across the liberal arts—grounded in responsible, human-centered AI that enhances (not replaces) academic rigor, critical thinking, and creativity.
**AI Policy & Guidelines**
Morehouse’s AI policies and guidelines prioritize integrity, transparency, and mission-aligned learning.
During the 2024–2025 academic year, Academic Affairs established an Academic Policy Working Group to develop generative AI guidance for both student learning and faculty instruction, with representation across the institution (including HR and IT).
