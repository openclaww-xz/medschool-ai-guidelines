RESPONSIBLE USE OF
GENERATIVE AI WITH F.A.S.T.E.R.
Source: Government of Canada, Guide on the Use of Generative AI


                              FAIR
                              Ensure content does not
                              include or amplify biases and
                              that it complies with human
                              rights, accessibility, and
                              procedural and substantive
                              fairness obligations.



                              ACCOUNTABLE
                              Take responsibility for the
                              content generated. This
                              includes making sure it is
                              factual, legal, ethical, and
                              compliant with the terms of
                              use.


                              SECURE
                              Ensure that the infrastructure
                              and tools are appropriate for
                              the security classification of
                              the information and that
                              privacy and personal
                              information are protected.


                              TRANSPARENT
                              Identify content that has been
                              produced using GAI; notify
                              users that they are interacting
                              with an AI tool; document
                              decisions.




                              EDUCATED
                              Learn about strengths, limitations,
                              and responsible use; learn how
                              to create effective prompts and
                              to identify potential weaknesses
                              in the outputs.



                              RELEVANT
                              Ensure use of GAI tools supports
                              user and organizational needs
                              and contributes to improved
                              outcomes; AI tools aren’t the
                              best choice in every situation.
