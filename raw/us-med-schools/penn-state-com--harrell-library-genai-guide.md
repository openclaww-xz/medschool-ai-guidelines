AI vs. GenAI

Artificial Intelligence is “a machine-based system that can, for a given set of human-defined objectives, make predictions, recommendations or decisions influencing real or virtual environments.”

GenAI (Generative AI) “refers to deep-learning models that can generate high-quality text, images, and other content based on the data they were trained on.”

Definitions from the Penn State
Final Report of the Joint Standing Committee on Effective and Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence in Higher Education Instruction

Penn State Generative AI Resources

Microsoft Copilot
is available to Penn State students, faculty and staff as part of the University's Microsoft contract and is the generative AI tool recommended for course-related use.
NOTE
: PSU Copilot is not HIPAA compliant.  College of Medicine employees and students should consult with CoM IT for specific guidance and consider using the CoM instance of M365 if handling PHI information.

AI Studio
, a suite of AI assistant and agent tools from various platforms, is available to University faculty and staff.  At present, Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT are available in the studio.  Penn State's AI guidance provides
support in selecting a tool
for different applications.
