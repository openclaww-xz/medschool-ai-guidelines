Welcome to the Official TTUHSC Artificial Intelligence (AI) Resource Page

Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools are rapidly evolving and offer powerful opportunities to support education, research, clinical care, and operations. This page serves as the official source for TTUHSC AI governance, policies, guidance, training, and related resources. TTUHSC is committed to ensuring AI is used responsibly, ethically, and in compliance with institutional and regulatory requirements. This page will be updated as AI technologies, institutional guidance, and applicable laws and regulations continue to evolve.

TTUHSC’s AI guidance is designed to:
Promote responsible and ethical AI use
Protect personal, confidential, and regulated information
Ensure compliance with institutional policies and legal requirements
Support innovation while minimizing risk
AI is a tool to support your work, not replace professional judgment.
AI Use Guidelines
Allowable Use

AI tools may be used when appropriate and in compliance with institutional policy. Publicly available information may be entered into AI tools, provided its use aligns with applicable guidelines and data governance requirements.

Prohibited Use

Using unapproved AI tools with sensitive or regulated data TTUHSC Data Classifications OP 56.04 CONTACT US FOR PDF

Entering confidential information into unapproved AI systems

Generating non-public or restricted content such as:
    Research data
    Legal analysis
    Grading decisions
    Personnel decisions

Engaging in fraudulent, illegal, or policy-violating activities

AI Governance at TTUHSC

The AI Governance and Risk Committee, established by the President’s Cabinet, provides oversight of AI activities to ensure alignment with TTUHSC’s mission, regulatory compliance, ethical standards, and risk management. The cross-functional committee approves AI policies, reviews and assesses AI tools and associated risks, monitors performance and compliance, evaluates vendors, oversees training, and addresses reported concerns.

TTUHSC is committed to protecting the privacy of patients, learners, and team members. Approved AI tools that meet institutional privacy and security standards are currently under evaluation. Until formal approval is confirmed, AI tools must not be used with protected or confidential information without prior review.

AI Literacy Training

TTUHSC's AI Literacy Training is available to all TTUHSC team members through ACME. The training provides an overview of artificial intelligence, responsible AI use, applicable institutional policies, ethical considerations, privacy and security expectations, and Texas state requirements related to AI. Completion of this training helps ensure team members understand their responsibilities when using AI technologies in the workplace. This training has been certified by the Texas Department of Information Resources (DIR) and satisfies the State of Texas AI literacy training requirements for TTUHSC team members.

Accessing the Training:
Log in to ACME using your eRaider credentials.
Select "Responsible AI Use" Training.
Launch and complete the course and quiz to receive credit.
