Generative AI Guidelines & Risks of Alternative Tools

Posted: February 6, 2025

A new generative AI model,
DeepSeek R1
, has been making headlines, but before using any AI tool for university-related work, it’s important to understand the risks.

Tufts Technology Services (TTS) reminds the community to follow our
Generative AI Guidelines
, which emphasize:

Approved Tools
– Use
university-supported AI tools
that comply with security and privacy policies.

Data Privacy Risks
– Many AI models, including DeepSeek R1, may store or use your inputs, creating potential risks for sensitive data.

Intellectual Property Concerns
– AI-generated content may have copyright implications or misinformation risks.

For safe and responsible AI use, refer to our
Tufts Generative AI Guidelines
and understand the
associated costs and risks.
