Faculty Guide to Using Generative AI at Tulane

Generative AI (GenAI) offers numerous benefits for research and scholarly work, but it also introduces ethical, legal, and professional risks, including plagiarism, data fabrication, bias, and potential policy violations. We offer guidelines and best practices for the responsible use of GenAI, emphasizing the importance of ongoing awareness of technological, legal, and field-specific developments, especially for students. Tulane University encourages faculty to stay informed about GenAI tools and participate in professional development.
