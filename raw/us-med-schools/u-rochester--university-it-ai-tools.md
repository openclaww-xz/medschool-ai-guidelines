Artificial Intelligence (AI) Tools

The University of Rochester offers secure, institutionally supported AI tools for faculty, staff, and students. The tools below comply with University privacy and data protection standards applicable to data within the risk category indicated for each, helping ensure your data remains secure and is not used to train external AI models.
