AI Policies and Expectations

The use of generative AI at UAB currently is governed by
existing university and health system policies
. These policies continue to apply when AI tools are used and provide the foundation for responsible, consistent, and compliant adoption across the enterprise.

UAB applies established policies related to data protection, academic integrity, research conduct, clinical care, procurement, and intellectual property to ensure AI use remains aligned with institutional standards, professional responsibilities, and regulatory requirements.

These policies collectively define:

Expectations for how UAB data must be protected and classified

Standards that uphold academic, research, and clinical integrity

Requirements for procurement, contracting, tool, system, and project approval

Expectations for authorship, attribution, and intellectual property

Together, they ensure AI use at UAB supports innovation while preserving human judgment, professional responsibility, and institutional trust.
