Artificial intelligence
(AI) involves the use of computers to accomplish tasks that usually require human intelligence. This broad definition refers to AI algorithms that classify, analyze, and draw predictions from data.

The University of Florida and the College of Medicine are committed to an ethical and equitable approach to AI in teaching, research, and business practices. AI has a crucial role in the education of future physicians, medical research, and treatment of patients.

The UF COM requires the use of AI in a responsible, ethical, and
professional
manner. Students should refer to course syllabi or the course director prior to usage of AI. Improper use or acknowledgement of AI for course assignments will be construed as receiving unauthorized assistance from another entity or individual in violation of the
UF Honor Code
.
