AI Guidance for Students

There is no single university-wide policy that determines when AI may be used for coursework. Expectations vary by instructor, assignment, department, and college.
Before using AI for any assignment, review your course syllabus, assignment instructions, and any guidance provided by your instructor. If you're unsure whether AI is permitted, ask before submitting your work. Unauthorized use of AI may be considered academic misconduct and could result in serious academic consequences.
Your Responsibilities
As a student, you are responsible for:
Understanding your instructor's expectations for AI use.
Following course and assignment-specific policies.
Completing your own academic work unless AI use has been authorized.
Citing or acknowledging AI use when required.
Reviewing AI-generated content for accuracy.
Protecting sensitive, personal, and university information.
AI is a rapidly developing technology. You should regularly check on university, collegiate, and instructor guidance to keep up with changes.

Academic Integrity

The University of Iowa's Academic Misconduct Policy prohibits the unauthorized use of artificial intelligence tools during academic exercises.
Using AI in ways that are not permitted by your instructor or course policies may constitute academic misconduct.
