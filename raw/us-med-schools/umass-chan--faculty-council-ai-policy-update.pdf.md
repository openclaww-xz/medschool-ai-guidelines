AI Policy Update to the
    Faculty Council
                    May 7, 2026
    Bill Barnett, Chief Research Computing Officer
Framing the UMass Chan AI Policy
 Context for this policy
 • Even though most policy AI statements are addressed in other policies, it is
   worth specifying them in AI policies to ensure alignment in the context of AI
 What is the intent
 • Very general statements that align with broad principles and have application
   across the mission and administrative areas.
 Why are we reviewing
 • For transparency, to ensure faculty perspectives are heard, and for alignment
   with existing faculty policies on AI.

Main Stipulations
     Use internal AI tools only with data for which they are approved
     • So internal AI tools are used only with data from approved security levels

     Do not use external tools for UMass Chan content
     • So UMass Chan content and IP is kept private, and not exposed to corporate data mining

     Ensure Software with AI components is reviewed

     • So our data are protected in software applications that increasingly have AI components.

     Review Content you use AI tools to generate
     • So everyone knows they are accountable for their use of AI generated content.

     Insure you are using AI tools Ethically and Responsibly
     • So everyone knows they are responsible to behave ethically with AI
