Artificial Intelligence with Meaning

UT Southwestern is embracing AI with Meaning (AIM), using artificial intelligence to transform every part of academic medicine — improving how we diagnose disease, accelerate research, manage hospital operations, and enhance the patient experience.

At UT Southwestern, we are championing AIM as a systemwide tool to enhance clinical decision-making, accelerate scientific discovery, and improve efficiency, sustainability, and equity of healthcare delivery. AIM’s true value comes from responsible integration across disciplines:

In
clinical care
, it supports diagnosis, treatment, and care coordination.

In
research
, it helps uncover insights in large, complex datasets.

In
education
, it personalizes learning experiences and enhances simulation-based training for students.

In
administration
, it streamlines operations, reduces waste, and improves access for patients and staff.

UT Southwestern’s commitment to protecting private information

UT Southwestern is fully committed to protecting patient, student, employee, and other personal data in compliance with the
Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act
(HIPAA),
Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act
(FERPA), and other applicable privacy regulations. UT Southwestern has deployed strong security and privacy controls to protect confidential data. When it comes to AI tools, we need your help to continue that commitment.
