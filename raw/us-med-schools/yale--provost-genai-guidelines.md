# Guidelines for the Use of Generative AI Tools

Wednesday, September 20, 2023

[Summary: The following message provides guidance to all members of the Yale community regarding the responsible use of generative AI.]

The field of generative AI is developing rapidly. While its precise impacts are unknown, this technology will transform how we learn, teach, conduct research, and carry out daily tasks.
